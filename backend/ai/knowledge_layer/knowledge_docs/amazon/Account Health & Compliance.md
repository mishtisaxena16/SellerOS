# **SellerOS Account Health & Compliance Knowledge Base: Amazon.in**

## **Account Health and Performance Standards**

Amazon evaluates seller performance based on specific operational targets and policy adherence. These metrics are visible on the **Account Health page**, which serves as a central hub for monitoring compliance and taking corrective action before violations impact selling privileges.

### **Key Performance Metrics**

To maintain a healthy account, sellers must meet the following minimum targets:

- **Order Defect Rate (ODR):** A measure of customer service quality, including negative feedback and A-to-z Guarantee claims. The target is **below 1%**.
- **Late Shipment Rate (LSR):** The percentage of orders confirmed for shipment after the expected ship date. The target is **below 4%**.
- **Pre-Fulfillment Cancellation Rate:** The percentage of orders cancelled by the seller before shipment. The target is **below 2.5%**.
- **Service Quality Score (SQS):** A comprehensive operational score out of 100 points, calculated based on the following four metrics:
 - **Total Cancellation Rate (TCR):** Includes both seller-initiated and Amazon-automated cancellations.
 - **Total Late Dispatch Rate (TLDR):** Orders ship-confirmed after the expected date.
 - **Seller Controllable Return Rate (SCRR):** Orders returned or refunded due to defects, damage, sizing issues, poor quality, or descriptions that do not match the product.
 - **Out-of-Stock (OOS) Rate:** The number of times customers viewed products that were unavailable for purchase.

### **Account Health Rating (AHR)**

The AHR is a color-coded score that reflects the overall health of a selling account based on policy compliance. If this rating falls below the required threshold, the account is at risk of immediate deactivation.

### **Customer Service and Voice of the Customer (VOC)**

- **Voice of the Customer:** Sellers should use VOC tools to identify product quality or fulfillment issues highlighted by buyers.
- **Customer Service Quality Insights:** A dashboard for self-ship sellers tracking the **Buyer Contact Rate**, **Average Contact Response Time**, and **Buyer Dissatisfaction Rate**. While these insights are educational and do not currently impact account health, they represent opportunities to improve the customer experience.

## **Seller Code of Conduct and Ethical Requirements**

The Seller Code of Conduct requires all participants to act fairly and honestly. Violations may result in listing cancellations, the permanent withholding of payments, and removal of selling privileges.

### **Fair Dealing and Integrity**

- **Accurate Information:** Sellers must provide truthful information to Amazon and customers, including using an accurate business name and listing products in the correct categories.
- **Marketplace Fairness:** Prohibited activities include manipulating sales rank through fake orders, artificially inflating web traffic using bots, and attempting to damage other sellers' ratings or listings.
- **Single Account Policy:** Sellers may only maintain one Seller Central account per region unless there is a legitimate business need (e.g., owning multiple distinct brands) and all accounts are in good standing.

### **Communication and Review Policies**

- **Authorized Channels:** All contact with customers must go through **Buyer-Seller Messaging** and must be limited to order fulfillment or customer service. Marketing communications are strictly prohibited.
- **Review Integrity:** Sellers may not influence ratings or reviews. Prohibited actions include paying for reviews, offering incentives (like free products or coupons) for review removal, and soliciting reviews only from customers who had positive experiences.

### **Intellectual Property (IP) and Authenticity**

- **Brand Protection:** Enrolling in the **Amazon Brand Registry** helps protect trademarks and IP.
- **Transparency Program:** A program designed to identify and prevent the sale of counterfeit products using unique codes.
- **Infringement Reporting:** Sellers cannot file IP infringement notices against others if the primary goal is to benefit their own selling account (e.g., by removing a competitor).

## **Common Policy Violations**

The following actions frequently result in warnings, listing suppression, or account suspension:

- Selling counterfeit or inauthentic products.
- Manipulating customer reviews or ratings.
- Creating duplicate product listings.
- Providing inaccurate product information.
- Late shipment confirmations.
- High cancellation rates.
- Repeated customer complaints.
- Selling restricted or prohibited products.
- Misusing Buyer-Seller Messaging for marketing.
- Creating multiple seller accounts without approval.

## **Product and Listing Compliance**

Amazon monitors listings to ensure they provide a clear and consistent experience. Listings that fail to meet these standards may be suppressed from search results.

### **Listing Standards and Prohibitions**

- **Prohibited Content:** Descriptions, titles, and images must not contain pornographic, obscene, or offensive material.
- **Restricted Information:** Listings cannot include phone numbers, email addresses, website URLs, or promotional text (e.g., "clearance sale," "Best Seller").
- **HTML and Executables:** Detail pages must not contain HTML or JavaScript, with the exception of simple line breaks.

### **Preventing Listing Suppression**

Common reasons for a listing being hidden from search results include:

- **Missing Attributes:** Failure to provide a category (item-type), brand, or bullet points.
- **Image Violations:** Every listing requires a MAIN image on a pure white background. Images must not be blurry, pixelated, or include watermarks/logos.
- **Title Length:** Titles exceeding 200 characters will be suppressed regardless of category. For better visibility, parent titles should be under 80 characters.
- **Invalid Product IDs:** Amazon verifies UPCs against the **GS1 database**. UPCs that are not authentic or do not match the GS1 records will lead to suppression.

### **Safety and Legal Compliance**

- **Product Safety:** All products must conform to current safety standards.
- **Restricted Products:** Items such as weapons, narcotics, and certain animals are strictly prohibited.
- **Recalls:** Sellers are responsible for monitoring and responding to public or private product recalls and must notify Amazon immediately upon learning of one.

## **Claims, Returns, and Financial Compliance**

### **Amazon A-to-z Guarantee**

This guarantee protects customers regarding the timely delivery and condition of self-fulfilled orders. Sellers are required to:

- **Respond Promptly:** Reply to customer messages regarding delivery or returns within **48 hours**.
- **Resolve Directly:** Resolving issues through Buyer-Seller Messaging before a claim is filed protects the ODR.
- **Automatic Grants:** Amazon may automatically grant claims in favor of the customer if the seller fails to respond within the 48-hour window, provides no tracking, or misses the estimated delivery date.

### **SAFE-T Claims and Returns**

- **SAFE-T Claims:** Sellers can file these claims if they believe Amazon has wrongly debited their account for a refund (e.g., returnless refunds for items that should have been returned).
- **Proof of Delivery (OTP):** For certain returns handled by Amazon Transportation Services (ATS), sellers must use a **One-Time Password (OTP)** to confirm receipt. Failure to use this mechanism may invalidate future SAFE-T claims for lost packages.
- **Secured Returns:** High-risk returns are delivered in tamper-evident packaging. Sellers should inspect these for a **pink line** or "BAG TAMPERED" message before acceptance.

### **Regulatory and Tax Compliance**

- **E-Invoicing:** Eligible FBA sellers must generate and share an **Invoice Reference Number (IRN)** through the government portal 24 hours before pickup. Failure to comply can result in the suspension of pickup privileges.
- **Merchant Tax Report (MTR):** Sellers must use the MTR to ensure accurate tax filing, particularly for B2B transactions where invoice-level reporting is required.

## **Troubleshooting**

### **Account Health Rating Dropped**

Possible reasons include:

- Increased Order Defect Rate.
- Late shipment confirmations.
- High cancellation rate.
- Policy violations.
- Customer complaints.

### **Listing Suppressed**

Possible reasons:

- Missing product attributes.
- Invalid images.
- Title violations.
- Missing GS1 verification.
- Restricted product policy.

### **Account Suspension**

Recommended actions:

- Review Account Health notifications.
- Identify the violated policy.
- Prepare a detailed Plan of Action (POA).
- Submit supporting documents if requested.
- Monitor appeal status through Seller Central.

## **Enforcement, Appeals, and Reinstatement**

### **Account Suspension and Payment Holds**

Amazon may suspend an account or withhold payments if:

- The Account Health Rating falls below required thresholds.
- The account is linked to deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity (such as selling counterfeit goods).
- Performance issues create a financial risk to Amazon or third parties.

### **The Appeals Process**

- **30-Day Window:** Sellers generally have **30 calendar days** to appeal a granted A-to-z claim or a decision related to FBA shipment problems.
- **Plan of Action (POA):** Reinstatement of privileges often requires a review of the violation and a formal acknowledgement or POA that addresses the root cause of the non-compliance.

### **Security Compliance**

- **Two-Step Verification:** This is a mandatory security requirement for accessing Seller Central to protect account data and integrity.
- **Confidentiality:** Sellers must keep customer personal data confidential and use it only for order fulfillment or customer service. Misuse of data is a breach of the Business Solutions Agreement.

## **Best Practices**

- Monitor Account Health weekly.
- Respond to buyer messages within 24 hours whenever possible.
- Ship orders before the Estimated Ship Date.
- Keep inventory updated.
- Use valid tracking for every shipment.
- Review Voice of the Customer regularly.
- Avoid policy violations by reviewing Amazon policy updates.
- Maintain accurate product listings.
- Enable Two-Step Verification.
- Keep tax and business documents updated.

> **Important**
>
> Amazon continuously monitors seller performance, customer satisfaction, and policy compliance. Repeated violations, fraudulent activity, or failure to resolve performance issues may result in listing suppression, temporary suspension, permanent account deactivation, or withholding of funds.
