# **Amazon Advertising and Growth Knowledge Base**

## **Amazon Advertising Solutions**

Amazon provides three primary self-service advertising solutions to reach and engage shoppers at different stages of the purchase journey.

### **Sponsored Products**

* **Definition:** These are ads for individual product listings on Amazon designed to drive product visibility and sales.
* **Placement:** Ads appear on search results pages and product detail pages.
* **Growth Impact:** Sponsored Product ads are a key tool for driving discovery. Investing at least ₹5,000 in these ads can help products appear in high-visibility areas and, for new sellers, is associated with a 12x higher likelihood of reaching ₹1 lakh in gross sales within nine weeks.

### **Sponsored Brands**

* **Definition:** These ads showcase a brand’s portfolio rather than just a single item.
* **Features:** They feature the brand logo, a custom headline, and up to three specific products.
* **Placement:** Typically appear as search-result ads.

### **Sponsored Display**

* **Function:** Used alongside Sponsored Products and Brands to boost sales and enhance brand visibility through refined targeting and different creative formats.

### **Amazon Stores**

* **Definition:** Custom, multipage shopping destinations within Amazon for individual brands.
* **Benefits:** Allows brands to share their story and full product offering without requiring prior website design experience.

### **Support and Resources**

* **Advertiser Support:** Questions regarding campaign performance, budget optimization, and Brand Stores are handled by the Amazon Advertiser Support team, available via live chat or email in over 12 languages.
* **Learning:** Seller University provides a dedicated curriculum (Course 10: Advertise with Amazon) covering positioning, bidding, budgeting, and data analysis to improve ad performance.

### **Advertising Campaign Common Mistakes**

- Setting very low daily budgets.
- Using broad keywords without monitoring performance.
- Ignoring negative keywords.
- Running campaigns without optimizing listings first.
- Stopping campaigns before collecting enough performance data.

## **Listing Optimization for Better Conversions**

Optimizing listings is critical as it provides the most effective way for buyers to learn about products and influences their buying motivation.

### **Core Optimization Strategy**

* **Relevance:** Providing relevant and complete information increases product visibility and sales.
* **The First 90 Days:** The initial three months are vital for establishing listing practices that boost long-term performance.
* **A+ Content:** Brand owners can use A+ Content to add videos, enhanced images, and comparison charts to inform and inspire customers.

### **Product Titles**

* **Searchability:** Every individual word in a title is searchable.
* **Length Standards:** Titles should be clearly written. For non-media listings, parent titles with fewer than 80 characters are recommended to improve the shopping experience.
* **Formatting:** Capitalize the first letter of every word (maximum 50 characters for some formats).
* **Prohibitions:** Titles must not include promotional information (e.g., "clearance sale," "limited time offer") or subjective/time-sensitive comments (e.g., "Best Seller," "Extra Value").

### **Bullet Points**

* **Function:** Highlight the five key features customers should consider (e.g., dimensions, age appropriateness, skill level, country of origin).
* **Formatting:** Use sentence fragments, start with capital letters, and avoid ending punctuation. Use Arabic numerals (2 instead of two) and write out measurement units.
* **Constraints:** Each bullet should generally be under 200 characters (maximum 500 characters).
* **Prohibitions:** Do not include price, shipping information, or promotional text.

### **Search Terms and Keywords**

* **Generic Words:** Search terms should only include generic words that enhance discoverability.
* **Bytes Limit:** Keep search terms under 250 bytes.
* **Best Practices:** Use synonyms, abbreviations, and scenes; do not repeat words already in the title.
* **Technical Handling:** Use a single space to separate keywords; do not use commas, semicolons, or dashes. Amazon's system automatically handles misspellings and variants of spacing or pluralization.

### **Product Images**

* **Resolution:** Images must be professional photographs at least 1,600 pixels on the longest side to support the zoom feature.
* **Main Image Standards:** Must have a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255\) and accurately represent only the product for sale. The product should fill 80% to 85% of the frame.
* **Prohibited Elements:** Main images cannot include Amazon logos/trademarks (e.g., "Amazon’s Choice," "Prime"), watermarks, text, borders, or props that might confuse the customer.
* **Category-Specific Rules:**
 * **Clothing:** Women’s and Men’s clothing must be on a human model; Kids' and Baby clothing must be shown flat.
 * **Shoes:** Main image must show a single shoe facing left at a 45-degree angle.

### **Listing Optimization Common Mistakes**

- Using incomplete or inaccurate product information.
- Writing promotional titles instead of descriptive titles.
- Uploading low-quality or non-compliant product images.
- Ignoring backend search terms.
- Failing to optimize listings during the first 90 days.

## **Promotions and Sales Growth Tools**

### **General Promotions**

Promotions provide incentives for consumers to make buying decisions sooner or choose a specific seller.

* **Manage Promotions Tool:** Allows the creation of money-off discounts, free shipping, buy-one-get-one (BOGO) offers, and free-product offers.
* **Claim Codes:** Sellers can link promotions to marketing campaigns using custom claim codes.
* **Qualifications and Benefits:** Rules determine eligibility (e.g., minimum purchase quantity) and the resulting benefit (e.g., fixed discount or percentage off).

### **Coupons and Deals**

* **Coupons:** Digital coupons allow sellers to offer percentage or money-off discounts directly on the search results or product pages.
* **Lightning Deals:** These are top-tier deals featured on Amazon that can significantly increase brand visibility and sales.
* **Sales Event Planner:** A tool used to identify top deals and refine targeting for major events like Prime Day.

### **Prime Day Strategies**

* **Nature:** A two-day sale event exclusively for Prime members.
* **Seller Participation:** Prime sellers can participate to push business growth; for example, over 10 lakh Prime members shopped from small businesses during the lead-up to Prime Day 2020\.
* **Advantages:** A Prime badge increases product visibility and assures customers of quality, fast delivery, and reliable support.

### **Cashback Promotions Tool**

* **Function:** Simplifies end-to-end management of cashback incentives for products listed on Amazon.in.
* **Opt-In Process:** Sellers determine the cashback amount and the duration of the promotion.
* **Financials:** Sellers fund the entire cashback amount. Amazon initially bears and applies the amount at purchase, then adjusts/sets off these amounts from the seller's account balance.

### **Product Sampling Program**

* **Overview:** Offers relevant buyers a chance to try products for ₹1 to reduce friction and generate early reviews.
* **Benefits:** Assists in new product testing, brand awareness, and identifying lucrative markets for expansion.
* **Targeting:** Amazon distributes claim codes to a selected group of buyers based on past activity to encourage repeat buys and cross-selling.

### **Promotion Management Common Mistakes**

- Creating promotions without eligibility rules.
- Running excessive discounts that reduce profit margins.
- Forgetting promotion end dates.
- Combining conflicting promotions.

## **Business Expansion and Analysis**

### **Product Opportunity Explorer**

A tool that assists sellers in identifying new product opportunities and understanding customer demand to expand their selection.

### **B2B Central**

* **Growth Management:** Provides a focal point to manage the business-to-business (B2B) channel.
* **Sales Snapshot:** Allows comparison of B2B sales growth (from registered business customers) versus non-B2B sales.
* **B2B Performance by Industry:** Breaks down sales by industry and sub-industry segments to identify expansion opportunities.
* **Pricing:** Sellers can offer exclusive Business Prices and Quantity Discounts to registered business customers.

### **Business Planner**

* **Goal Setting:** Allows sellers to create goals (e.g., "Increase sales through marketing" or "Improve traffic through impressions") across different timeframes.
* **Tailored Recommendations:** Provides actionable steps, such as refining targeting or testing creatives, based on the performance of similar seller cohorts.

### **Manage Your Experiments**

A tool that facilitates A/B testing for product listings to determine which content (e.g., titles, images) performs better for conversion.

## **Performance Analysis and Metrics**

### **Sales and Traffic Metrics**

* **Net Sales Volume:** The total value of products sold at the listed price, including shipping and gift wrap, but minus promotional discounts, returns, and cancellations.
* **Net Sales Units:** The total number of products sold, excluding returns or cancellations.
* **Out-of-Stock (OOS) Rate:** The percentage of customer views where a product was out of stock. A high OOS rate reduces Buy Box win chances and overall visibility.
* **Impressions:** Tracks how often ads or listings are seen by customers.

### **Advertising Performance (ACoS and ROAS)**

Specific ad performance metrics are monitored through the Amazon Ads dashboard, allowing sellers to adjust budgets and targeting based on ROI.

### **Operational Growth Metrics (STEP)**

The STEP program levels (Basic to Premium) are determined by operational performance, which impacts eligibility for growth benefits.

* **Prime Listing Customer Views %:** Percentage of total views where a product had a Prime offering.
* **Regional In-Stock %:** Percentage of orders where inventory was within 300km of the customer at the time of ordering.

### **Advertising Performance Best Practices**

- Monitor campaign performance regularly.
- Optimize bids using ACoS and ROAS.
- Pause underperforming keywords.
- Increase budgets only for profitable campaigns.
- Continuously test new creatives and keywords.

## **Policies and Best Practices**

### **Seller Code of Conduct**

* **Fairness:** Sellers must act fairly and honestly. Prohibited activities include manipulating sales rank through fake orders or artificially inflating web traffic using bots.
* **Review Policy:** Sellers may not influence customer reviews by paying for them or offering incentives (like coupons or free products) in exchange for positive feedback.
* **Communications:** Marketing communications to customers are strictly prohibited; all contact must go through Buyer-Seller Messaging and be related to order fulfillment or customer service.

### **Important Advertising Policy Notes**

Amazon prohibits artificial traffic generation, fake orders, click manipulation, and incentivized reviews. Violations may result in campaign suspension, listing suppression, or account deactivation.

### **Preventing Listing Suppression**

Amazon hides listings that do not meet quality standards. Common reasons for suppression include:

* **Missing Attributes:** Lack of values in required fields like "Category (product type)" or "Department".
* **Title Issues:** Parent titles exceeding characters limits or being incomplete.
* **Image Violations:** Failure to comply with main image standards (e.g., non-white backgrounds).
* **Identification Errors:** Lack of valid UPCs or using UPCs that do not match the GS1 database.

### **Buy Box (Featured Offer) Optimization**

* **Significance:** The Featured Offer is the primary section where customers "Add to Cart.".
* **Winning Strategies:** Maintain competitive pricing, high listing quality, and consistent in-stock status. Using the Automate Pricing tool can improve the chances of being selected as the Featured Offer.

### **Best Practices for Expansion**

* **New Seller Incentives:** New sellers can unlock up to ₹41,000 in incentives, including ad credits and fee waivers, to accelerate their initial growth.
* **Catalog Selection:** Bringing more selection to the catalog is a key lever for increasing customer engagement and total net sales.
* **Demand Analysis:** Regularly evaluate demand (especially for FBA products) to align inventory levels with market needs.
