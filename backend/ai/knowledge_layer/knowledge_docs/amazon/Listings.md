# **Amazon Product Listing and Optimization Knowledge Base**

## **Product Identification and Core Listing Methods**

Product listings provide the essential information customers need to evaluate a product and make a buying decision. Every product on Amazon is identified by specific codes, including the **ASIN** (Amazon Standard Identification Number), **UPC**, **EAN**, **ISBN**, or **GTIN**.

### **Methods for Adding Products**

There are two primary ways to showcase products on the marketplace:

1. **Matching an Existing Listing:** If a product is already available on Amazon, sellers can add a new offer by matching the existing product detail page.
2. **Creating a New Listing:** For products not yet listed on Amazon, sellers must create a new listing by uploading complete product information. Listings can also be created and edited via the Amazon Seller App.

## **Product Title Requirements and Guidelines**

The product title is the first information customers see; it must clearly and concisely describe key product attributes to help customers identify and compare items.

### **Technical and Content Requirements**

- **Character Limit:** Titles should remain within the recommended length to improve readability on search results and mobile devices.
- **Special Characters:** Do not use special characters such as `!`, `$`, `?`, `_`, `{`, `}`, `^`, `¬`, or `¦`. Standard punctuation like hyphens, commas, and periods is allowed only when necessary.
- **Word Repetition:** A title must not contain the same word more than twice (e.g., repeating "Baby Boy" multiple times is non-compliant).
- **Prohibited Phrases:** Titles must not contain promotional language such as "free shipping," "100% quality guaranteed," or subjective claims like "Hot Item" or "Best Seller".
- **Formatting:** Use numerals (e.g., "2" instead of "two") and capitalize the first letter of each word except for prepositions, conjunctions, or articles.

### **Information Hierarchy and Highlights**

The optimal title structure should follow this order: **Brand Name \> Flavor or Style \> Product Type \> Key Attribute \> Color \> Size or Pack Count \> Model Number**.

For products requiring more detail than the 75-character limit allows, the **Item Highlights** field provides an additional 125 characters for materials or recommended use cases, which appear below the title in search results.

### **Product Title Common Mistakes**

- Exceeding the recommended title length.
- Repeating keywords multiple times (keyword stuffing).
- Using promotional phrases such as "Best Seller" or "Free Shipping".
- Writing titles entirely in uppercase.
- Including unsupported special characters.

## **Product Bullet Point Requirements**

Bullet points are concise statements that highlight key features and benefits, helping customers quickly determine if a product meets their needs.

### **Writing Standards**

- **Count and Length:** Include at least **three bullet points**, each ranging from 10 to 255 characters.
- **Structure:** Start each bullet with a header followed by a colon and a description (e.g., "Cotton fabric: Made from 100% cotton").
- **Formatting:** Capitalize the first letter, use sentence fragments, and **do not use end punctuation**.
- **Content Focus:** Highlight unique benefits rather than brand marketing stories or competitor comparisons.

### **Prohibited Content in Bullets**

Bullet points may be removed if they contain:

- Special characters, emojis, or placeholder text like "N/A" or "TBD".
- Unsubstantiated claims such as "eco-friendly" or "anti-bacterial" unless verifiable on packaging.
- Guarantee, warranty, or shipping information.
- External hyperlinks or contact information.

### **Bullet Point Common Mistakes**

- Writing long paragraphs instead of concise bullets.
- Repeating the product title.
- Including warranty or shipping information.
- Using vague marketing claims without evidence.

## **Product Descriptions and Quality Optimization**

Impactful descriptions present information interestingly, covering the **5Ws and 1H**: **What** the product is, **Who** it is for, **Why** it should be bought, **Where** it is used, **When** it is useful, and **How** it works.

### **Best Practices for Conversion**

- **Features vs. Benefits:** Focus on how features solve buyer problems (e.g., describing a comfortable sole rather than just listing its material).
- **Readability:** Use white space, bullet points, and bold text for key features to make the content scannable.
- **Influential Language:** Use persuasive words that stimulate senses (e.g., "smooth silk," "crunchy nuts").
- **Storytelling:** Humanize the brand by sharing what inspired the product or the obstacles overcome during its development.

## **Product Image Standards**

Images are essential for evaluation; every listing must have at least one compliant **Main Image**, with a recommendation of six additional images and one video.

### **Technical Specifications**

- **Format:** JPEG (.jpg) is preferred; TIFF, PNG, and non-animated GIFs are also accepted.
- **Dimensions:** Minimum 500 pixels and maximum 10,000 pixels on the longest side. A size of **1,000 pixels or larger** is required to enable the zoom feature.
- **Naming:** File names must consist of the product identifier (e.g., ASIN) \+ variant code \+ extension.

### **Main Image (Hero Image) Rules**

- **Background:** Must be **pure white** (RGB 255, 255, 255).
- **Composition:** The product should fill at least **85%** of the frame.
- **Exclusions:** No text, logos, watermarks, borders, mannequins, or props that are not included with the product.
- **Accuracy:** Must accurately represent the product’s color, scale, and quantity without using placeholders.

### **Product Image Common Mistakes**

- Uploading low-resolution images.
- Using non-white backgrounds for the main image.
- Adding watermarks or promotional text.
- Showing accessories that are not included.
- Cropping the product excessively.

## **Shoppable Video Guidelines**

Adding videos can increase sales by an average of 23.8% by demonstrating size, functionality, and brand story.

### **Technical Requirements for Video**

- **Format:** MOV or MP4, up to 1080p resolution, and a maximum size of **5 GB**.
- **Duration:** Between **1 and 12 minutes**.
- **Thumbnail:** High-resolution JPEG or PNG under 5 MB.

### **Content Types and Standards**

- **Types:** Product Overviews, Unboxing, How-to/Product Operation, Setup Guides, and Troubleshooting.
- **Restrictions:** No promotional calls to action, external links, unauthorized music, or visible third-party branding.

## **Product Attributes and Variations**

Attributes are specific facts unique to a product that drive search discoverability and power refinement filters.

### **Attribute Categories**

Attributes are prioritized in three views in Seller Central:

1. **Required:** Mandatory fields for listing creation.
2. **Recommended:** Information customers typically look for during evaluation.
3. **All:** The complete set of available technical attributes.

### **Parent-Child Variations**

Variations allow customers to choose between different sizes or colors of the same product.

- **Parent ASIN:** The non-buyable entity that holds the children together.
- **Child ASINs:** Specific buyable variations (e.g., "White, Medium").
- **Naming Policy:** Include size and color in the title of **child ASINs only**.

## **Search Optimization and Keyword Management**

Discovery occurs through browsing (category hierarchy) and searching (lexical matching).

### **Browse Classification**

Sellers must use **Item Type Keywords** to place products in the correct "browse nodes" or digital aisles. **Browse Tree Guides (BTG)** are category-specific documents that provide these keywords.

### **Generic Keywords (Search Terms)**

These are backend keywords not visible on the detail page that enhance discoverability.

- **Limit:** The Generic Keyword attribute is limited to less than **250 bytes**.
- **Best Practices:** Use synonyms, abbreviations, and lowercase letters.
- **Prohibitions:** Do not include brand names, ASINs, temporary statements (e.g., "new"), or punctuation marks.

### **Search Optimization Common Mistakes**

- Repeating keywords already present in the title.
- Adding competitor brand names.
- Exceeding the 250-byte limit.
- Using punctuation unnecessarily.

## **A+ Content (Enhanced Brand Content)**

A+ Content allows brand owners to replace the plain text product description with rich marketing content, including comparison charts and brand stories.

### **Prerequisites and Eligibility**

- **Brand Registry:** The seller must be a brand representative or authorized reseller registered in the Amazon Brand Registry.
- **ASIN Registration:** ASINs must be registered to the brand and contain high-quality, compliant images.

### **Basic vs. Premium A+ Content**

- **Basic:** Allows up to **5 modules** and uses standard images (970x300) and text layouts.
- **Premium:** For eligible brands, offers up to **7 modules**, including interactive hotspots, multiple videos, and carousels.
- **Brand Story:** A separate carousel module appearing in the "From the brand" section with up to 19 cards.

## **Listing Compliance and Maintenance**

### **Prohibited and Restricted Products**

Certain items are prohibited from being listed on Amazon.in, including:

- Animals.
- Weapons.
- Narcotic drugs.

### **Listing Suppression and Troubleshooting**

Products may be suppressed from search results due to quality issues such as:

- **Missing Main Image:** If no compliant main image exists, the listing is hidden.
- **Title Violations:** Excessive repetition or promotional content in titles.
- **No Active Offer:** Listings with expired offers or zero quantity.
- **Missing Browse Node:** Products not assigned to a category "shelf" are not indexed.
- **Common Errors:** Error 90057 (invalid value), 90220 (missing required attribute), or 90225 (character limit exceeded).

>**Important Listing Compliance Notes **
>
> Amazon may suppress or remove listings that violate title, image, or product policy requirements. Repeated violations can negatively affect account health.
