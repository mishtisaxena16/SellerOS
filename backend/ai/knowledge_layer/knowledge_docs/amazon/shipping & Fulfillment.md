# **Amazon Shipping & Fulfillment Knowledge Base**

This document contains operational knowledge related to shipping, order fulfillment, packaging requirements, returns management, logistics programs, performance metrics, and shipping compliance for Amazon India sellers. It is intended to help answer seller questions regarding fulfillment workflows and shipping operations.

## **Overview of Fulfillment Channels**

Amazon India provides multiple fulfillment options depending on business needs, product type, and geographical reach.

### **Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)**

Under the FBA program, Amazon handles the storage, picking, packing, shipping, and customer service for products.

* **Storage:** Products are stored in Amazon Fulfillment Centers (FCs). Amazon maintains electronic records of inventory levels but is not required to physically mark units.
* **Shipping & Delivery:** Amazon ships units from its inventory to customers throughout India. These may be shipped alongside products from other merchants.
* **Customer Service:** Amazon is responsible for all customer service issues related to packaging, handling, shipment, and returns for FBA units.
* **Prime Eligibility:** Products in FBA are automatically eligible for the Prime badge, ensuring fast and free shipping for Prime members.

### **FBA Best Practices**

- Send inventory before peak sales periods.
- Label products correctly before shipping.
- Monitor storage limits regularly.
- Avoid sending damaged inventory.
- Replenish inventory before products become unavailable.

### **Amazon Easy Ship**

Easy Ship allows sellers to store and pack their own products while leveraging Amazon’s carrier network for delivery.

* **Workflow:** The seller stores products in their own warehouse, packs them, and schedules a pickup. An Amazon agent picks up the package and delivers it to the customer.
* **Reach:** Currently caters to 99.4% of pin codes in India.
* **Confirmation:** Shipment is automatically confirmed once the pickup associate scans the package; sellers do not need to manually confirm shipment in Seller Central.

### **Shipping Common Mistakes**

- Scheduling pickup after the Estimated Ship Date.
- Incorrect package dimensions.
- Forgetting to print shipping labels.
- Missing pickup appointments.

### **Self Ship / Merchant Fulfilled Network (MFN)**

For self-ship orders, the seller handles the entire fulfillment lifecycle independently.

* **Workflow:** The seller stores products, manages packaging, and utilizes their own delivery associates or third-party carriers for delivery.
* **Requirements:** Sellers must retrieve order information at least once every business day and provide tracking details for all shipments to ensure a positive customer experience.

### **Self Ship / Merchant Fulfilled Network Best Practices**

- Upload tracking immediately.
- Use reliable courier partners.
- Pack products securely.
- Ship before the Estimated Ship Date.

### **Seller Flex**

Seller Flex allows sellers to get FBA-like services and Prime eligibility while maintaining inventory in their own warehouse.

* **Benefits:** Sellers can join the program to gain Prime status and FBA-level logistics support for their independent inventory.

### **Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP)**

SFP is an invite-only program that allows sellers to display the Prime badge on Easy Ship products. It guarantees customers high-quality service, including fast delivery and reliable support.

## **Shipping Workflow and Order Fulfillment**

### **Order Information Retrieval**

Sellers are required to check Seller Central at least once every business day to retrieve current Order Information and shipping requirements.

### **Shipment Creation and Scheduling**

* **FBA Inbound:** Sellers send inventory to Amazon by creating a shipping plan, packing products, and using either the online shipment creation tool (for small orders) or the Inbound Shipment feed upload tool (for large orders).
* **Easy Ship Pickup:** Sellers can schedule pickups individually, in bulk via feeds, or through the Amazon Seller app.
* **Estimated Ship Date (ESD):** This is the date by which an order must be shipped. It is determined by the shipping availability period set by the seller or, if not specified, is generally two days (excluding Sundays and public holidays) after the order is placed.
* **Pickup Deadlines:** For Easy Ship, pickups must be scheduled such that they occur on or before the ESD. Time slots after the ESD are unavailable for scheduling.

### **Shipping Labels and Documentation**

* **Labels:** All units must comply with Amazon’s labeling requirements. For FBA, units received without a barcode label may incur unplanned prep fees.
* **Packing Slips:** Every shipment must include an order-specific packing slip.
* **Seller Identity:** Sellers must identify themselves as the "Seller of Record" on all packing slips and documentation.
* **Regulatory Forms:** Sellers are responsible for raising and providing all statutorily required documents, such as delivery challans, waybills, and stock transfer forms (e.g., Form F for FBA inbound).

### **Packaging Requirements**

* **Standard:** Sellers must package all products in a commercially reasonable manner to protect against damage and deterioration during shipment and storage.
* **FBA Inbound:** Units must be properly protected for transit. Improperly packaged or labeled units may be returned to the seller at their expense.

### **Workflow Overview**

Typical shipping process:

1. Customer places an order.
2. Seller receives the order.
3. Inventory is verified.
4. Product is packed.
5. Shipping label is generated.
6. Courier picks up the shipment.
7. Shipment is delivered.
8. Return or replacement is processed if required.

## **Weight and Dimension Policies**

* **Accurate Data:** For Easy Ship, sellers must provide accurate weight and dimensions during scheduling. Amazon allows edits within a "recommended range" based on packaging variations.
* **Audit and Errors:** Entering values outside the recommended range will prevent pickup scheduling. Providing incorrect weight and dimensions may result in additional audit fees.
* **Fee Impact:** Easy Ship fees are calculated based on weight slabs (the higher of dead weight or volumetric weight). If actual values change the weight slab, sellers should submit image proof through the appropriate form within 15 days.

### **Why Accurate Measurements Matter**

Incorrect package dimensions may:

- Increase shipping charges.
- Delay pickups.
- Trigger audit fees.
- Cause incorrect weight classification.

## **Returns, Refunds, and Replacements**

### **Return Policies**

* **Consistency:** Sellers must match or exceed Amazon’s standard return policy to ensure a consistent buyer experience.
* **Response Window:** Sellers are recommended to respond to return requests within 24 hours (required within 48 hours for self-fulfilled orders).
* **Return Labels:** When a return is approved, Amazon sends the buyer a label with the seller's return address. By default, these labels are not prepaid.

### **Refund Process**

* **MFN/Self-Ship:** Sellers determine and calculate refunds, including shipping and handling charges, and route them through Amazon.
* **FBA:** Amazon manages all returns and refunds for FBA orders.
* **Timing:** Sellers are encouraged to wait for the returned item to evaluate its condition before issuing a refund. Obligations for refunds must be met within 30 days of the request.

### **Order Replacements (Self-Fulfilled)**

* **Eligibility:** Buyers can choose an exact-item replacement if the item is in stock and return pickup is available in their area.
* **Workflow:** A replacement order is created in a "Pending" state and is only released for shipping once the carrier partner successfully picks up the original item from the buyer.
* **Costs:** There is no additional "Selling on Amazon" fee for fulfilling a replacement order, and the seller is not paid for the differential price.

### **Return Logistics (OTP & Secured Returns)**

* **OTP Verification:** For Easy Ship and Self-Ship returns handled by Amazon Transportation Services (ATS), sellers must provide a One-Time Password (OTP) to the delivery associate to confirm receipt of the return.
* **Secured Returns:** High-risk returns are delivered in tamper-evident packaging that changes color if opened. Sellers should check for a pink line or the words "BAG TAMPERED" on the seal before accepting the package.

### **Common Reasons for Returns**

- Damaged products
- Wrong product delivered
- Missing accessories
- Size mismatch
- Product not as described

## **Shipping and Delivery Performance Metrics**

Sellers must monitor key operational metrics to maintain account health and program eligibility.

### **Service Quality Score (SQS)**

Operational performance is measured out of 100 points, derived from the following metrics:

* **Total Late Dispatch Rate (TLDR):** The percentage of orders ship-confirmed after the expected ship date.
* **Total Cancellation Rate (TCR):** The percentage of orders cancelled by the seller or automatically by Amazon.
* **Seller Controllable Return Rate (SCRR):** The percentage of orders returned due to seller-related issues like defective products, sizing problems, or descriptions not matching the item.
* **Out-of-Stock (OOS) Rate:** The number of customer views on products that were out of stock as a percentage of total customer views.

### **Account Health Standards**

To avoid deactivation or suspension of shipping privileges, sellers should aim for:

* **Order Defect Rate (ODR):**

Measures the percentage of orders with negative customer experiences, including negative feedback, A-to-Z claims, or chargebacks.

Recommended target:
Less than 1%.
* **Pre-fulfillment Cancellation Rate:** \< 2.5%
* **Late Shipment Rate:** \< 4%

### **Other Metrics**

* **Prime Listing Customer Views %:** Tracks how often customers see Prime-eligible offers from the seller.
* **Regional In-Stock %:** The percentage of orders where the customer was within 300 km of the available inventory.
* **Buyer Contact Rate:** Tracks customer service contacts seeking refunds/replacements due to shipping or fulfillment problems.

## **Shipping Fees and Charges**

* **Easy Ship Fees:** Starts at ₹38 per item; Shipping fees vary depending on package size, weight, delivery distance, and current Amazon pricing policies. Sellers should verify current fee schedules inside Seller Central before calculating margins.
* **FBA Fees:** Includes fulfillment fees (picking, packing, shipping, customer service) and monthly inventory storage fees based on volume. FBA shipping fees start at ₹28 per item.
* **Self-Ship Costs:** Sellers incur the full cost of shipping through their chosen third-party carrier.
* **Cancellation Charges:** Sellers are charged a cancellation fee (100% to 150% of the referral fee) for orders cancelled for any reason other than a buyer request, or if shipping is not confirmed within 24 hours of the ESD.
* **Reimbursements:** Sellers are eligible for automatic referral and closing fee reimbursements for undeliverable or rejected shipments.

## **Important Compliance and Best Practices**

### **Shipping Restrictions**

* **Hazmat:** Sellers must follow strict guidelines for dangerous goods, including identification, documentation, and specific requirements for lithium batteries.
* **Unsuitable Units:** Amazon may refuse, return, or destroy FBA units that are defective, mislabeled, hazardous, or create safety risks.

### **Common Errors and Solutions**

* **Pickup Misses:** If an Easy Ship pickup is not attempted by 6:00 p.m. on the scheduled date, sellers should fill out the "Pickup Miss" form in Seller Central to protect their metrics.
* **Scheduling Errors:** Issues during scheduling should be resolved using the "Schedule pick-up errors and their fix" resource.
* **A-to-z Claims:** Sellers must respond to delivery-related messages within 48 hours to prevent claims from being automatically granted in favor of the customer.

### **Best Practices to avoid common errors**

* **Packaging:** Use secure, commercially reasonable packaging to minimize the Seller Controllable Return Rate.
* **Communication:** Only contact customers through Buyer-Seller Messaging and only for order fulfillment or customer service purposes.
* **Inventory Management:** Maintain sufficient stock levels to avoid a high Out-of-Stock rate, which reduces visibility and Buy Box winning chances.

### **Common Shipping Compliance Issues**

- Incorrect labels
- Missing invoices
- Hazardous goods shipped incorrectly
- Poor packaging
- Late shipment confirmation
- Wrong tracking numbers
- Frequent cancellations
