# Flipkart Restricted Products

## Overview

Flipkart prohibits the sale of certain products to ensure compliance with legal, safety, regulatory, and marketplace policies.

Listings that violate these policies may be blocked, removed, or lead to seller account actions.

---

## Major Prohibited Categories

### Alcohol & Liquor

Not allowed:

- Alcoholic beverages
- Beer, wine, spirits, liqueurs
- Alcohol promotion products
- Products encouraging alcohol consumption

---

### Animals & Wildlife Products

Not allowed:

- Live animals
- Wildlife products
- Ivory products
- Animal trophies
- Fur products
- Endangered species products
- Coral and marine wildlife products
- Snake venom products

---

### Restricted Content

Not allowed:

- Copyright-infringing content
- Pirated media
- Hacking materials
- Illegal databases
- Obscene or sexually explicit content
- Content promoting violence or hatred
- Incorrect maps of India
- Government IDs and licenses

---

### Cosmetics

Not allowed:

- Expired cosmetics
- Adulterated products
- Prescription-only cosmetic products
- Products containing restricted ingredients
- Tampered products
- Testers

---

### Drugs & Medical Products

Not allowed:

- Prescription drugs
- Controlled substances
- Narcotics
- Counterfeit medicines
- Veterinary prescription drugs
- Drug paraphernalia
- Miracle cure claims
- Products requiring medical supervision

---

### Electronic Nicotine Products

Not allowed:

- E-cigarettes
- Vape devices
- Vape refills
- Hookah accessories
- Electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS)

---

### Electronics & Communication Devices

Not allowed:

- Signal jammers
- Cable descramblers
- Surveillance devices (where restricted)
- Copyright bypass devices
- Illegal drone products
- Unauthorized signal boosters

---

### Explosives & Flammable Materials

Not allowed:

- Fireworks
- Explosives
- Ammunition components
- LPG transfer equipment
- Highly flammable substances

---

### Gambling Products

Not allowed:

- Lottery products
- Betting products
- Gambling-related items

---

### Hazardous Chemicals

Not allowed:

- Restricted pesticides
- Industrial chemicals
- Radioactive materials
- Unregistered insecticides
- Hazardous substances

---

### Illegal & Offensive Products

Not allowed:

- Human organs
- Stolen goods
- Offensive content
- Hate-promoting products
- Products violating Indian laws
- Misleading or harmful products

---

### Plants & Animals

Not allowed:

- Illegal seeds
- Restricted plant products
- Cannabis-related plants
- Live pets and animals

---

### Plastic & Thermocol Products

Not allowed:

- Single-use plastic products
- Plastic carry bags

- Thermocol disposable items

---

### Police & Military Products

Not allowed:

### Firearms & Weapons

- Ammunition

### Dangerous Weapons

### Intellectual Property Violations

Not allowed:

- Counterfeit products
- Trademark violations
- Copyright violations

---

### Restricted Territories

Products sourced from sanctioned or restricted regions may not be permitted.

---

### Food Products

Food products must comply with:

- FSSAI regulations
- BIS requirements

Not allowed:

- Expired food
- Unsafe food
- Adulterated food
- Misbranded food
- Non-compliant food products

---

## Seller Guidance

Before listing products:

1. Verify category eligibility.
2. Ensure compliance with Indian laws.
3. Avoid restricted or regulated products.
4. Ensure all certifications and approvals are available where required.
5. Review Flipkart's latest marketplace policies before listing.
