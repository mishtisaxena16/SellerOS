What is FSSAI?

FSSAI plays a pivotal role in shaping the health and safety landscape of the Indian food industry. As
the authoritative voice, FSSAI product approval is crucial for any food-related business aiming to
establish its credibility and ensure compliance with national health standards. It plays a pivotal role
in protecting consumer health by guiding and monitoring food safety measures, making its approval
essential for all food businesses.

The List of Documents Required for Central FSSAI Licence

The FSSAI product approval procedure begins with a fundamental step: ensuring you have the
necessary set of documents. This collection isn’t just a formality; it's the foundation of your
application, crucial for ensuring that everything runs smoothly and efficiently. It's essential to
approach this step methodically, as each document establishes your business's compliance with food
safety standards and readiness for the FSSAI approval process.

Business and Operational Details:

Form B duly completed and signed.

Plan of the processing unit showing dimensions and operation-wise area allocation.

List of Directors/Partners/Proprietor with address, contact details, and photo ID.

Name and list of equipment and machinery used with their number and installed capacity.

List of food categories to be manufactured.

Safety, Quality, and Compliance:

Authority letter from the manufacturer nominating a responsible person’s name and address.

Analysis report of water to be used in the process to confirm portability.

Source of raw material for milk, meat, etc.

Recall plan, wherever applicable.

Ministry of Commerce Certificate for 100% EOU.

NOC/PA document issued by FSSAI.

IE code document issued by DGFT.

Form IX.

Certificate from the Ministry of Tourism.

Legal, Property, and Manufacturer Details:

Proof of possession of premises

Partnership deed/affidavit of proprietorship.

NOC and copy of License from the manufacturer.

Food Safety Management and Declarations:

Food Safety Management System Plan or certificate.

NOC from the municipality or local body.

Declaration form.

Financial and Logistic Documentation:

Supporting document for proof of turnover and transportation.

What Steps Are Taken in the FSSAI Approval Process?

Beginning the FSSAI product approval process is much like preparing for a journey in the culinary
world, where adherence to standards is paramount. This path through a landscape of regulatory
compliance and quality assurance is much like a chef meticulously planning a meal from start to
finish.

Licence to Operate a Food Business

Obtaining a licence to operate a food business is the initial step toward FSSAI approval. This licence
certifies your business's adherence to the food safety norms and operational guidelines set by FSSAI.

Registration of Business

Business registration with FSSAI is a declaration of your commitment to food safety standards. It's
the first official step in your journey towards gaining consumer trust and regulatory compliance.

Product Labelling

Accurate product labelling is crucial in the FSSAI approval process. It's staying transparent in
communicating your consumers about product content, ensuring honesty and clarity in your
offerings.

Lab-Tested Products

Products undergoing rigorous laboratory testing are pivotal in meeting FSSAI's stringent safety
standards. These tests make sure your products are safe, wholesome, and fit for consumption.

Food Product Approval Application

A detailed application for each product, outlining its composition, manufacturing process, and safety
parameters, is a critical step in the FSSAI approval process.

Product Approval by the FSSAI

The culmination of the FSSAI approval process is the final product approval. This signifies your
product's compliance with all the safety and quality benchmarks set by the authority.
