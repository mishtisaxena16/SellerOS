How to Start an Online Selling Business

From expanding and reaching a larger target audience to improving engagement, e-commerce opens
up doors to a lot of possibilities for large and small-scale businesses. With that said, Flipkart Seller
Hub offers you the perfect opportunity to transform your company beyond a specific geographical
region or consumer base. If you are looking to set foot in the e-commerce industry, our
comprehensive online selling guide can help you get a headstart.

You might think that it is a lengthy process, but following a few easy steps will encourage your
business to grow exponentially. Let us find out how to start selling online on Flipkart Seller Hub.

How to Register on Flipkart Seller Hub?

Beginning your journey with Flipkart Seller Hub will only take up a few minutes of your time and can
reward you with several years of success. We have curated an eight-step guide to help you register
successfully on our platform. So, let us learn how to start selling online from home using Flipkart
Seller Hub.

Enter Contact Details

In order to register on Flipkart Seller Hub, go to “www.seller.flipkart.com” and click on the “Start
Selling” button. Enter your phone number and you will receive an OTP. Use the code to complete the
mobile verification. Then, proceed with entering your email address.

Enter GSTIN or PAN

Right below the contact details section, you will see the question, “What are you looking to sell on
Flipkart?” You are provided with two options — “All Categories” and “Only Books”. Select the
appropriate option as per your needs. If you choose the former, you must enter and verify your
GSTIN. On the other hand, selecting the latter would require PAN verification. After entering this
information, click on the “Register & Continue” button.

Create a Password

The next and one of the most critical steps of this online selling guide is creating a strong password of
at least eight characters. It should include an uppercase and a lowercase letter, along with a special
character. Lastly, enter your full name and display name, and click on the “Continue” button.

Now, you will be directed to a dashboard that displays the status of data that has been filled along
with the ones that are pending. The next few steps of this online selling guide will help you fill in
certain important information and complete your profile.

Verify Your Email Address

Just as you verified your mobile number, email verification is a quintessential step of this online
selling guide. To do so, go to the onboarding dashboard. Here, you will see a button beside your
email address. Upon clicking, you receive an email with a verification link. Simply click on it and your
email will be verified within seconds.

Enter Your Signature

This is one of the most crucial steps in this online selling guide. Your e-signature is just as significant
as your real one. Here, you will be provided with two options — “Draw” or “Create”. If you choose

the former option, you will have to manually draw your signature on your phone or computer. It is
ideal to use a mouse or stylus for this process.

On the other hand, the latter requires you to simply enter your name, and an e-signature will be
automatically created for you. Once you draw or create it, click on the “Add” button. Remember, your
e-signature will be used in all your invoices, so choose accordingly

Enter Store and Pickup Details

The details about your store and pickup location need to be accurate to ensure a hassle-free process
for the delivery executive. Note that the address and the pincode are essential pieces of information,
as your products will be picked up from this location.

You need to enter the name of the store and that of the owner. Then, come up with a concise and
appealing description of your store and enter the pin code in which your shop is located. After that,
provide the exact address of your store and click on the “Save” button.

Enter Bank Details

In this step of the online selling guide, you must provide information about the bank that is used for
your business. Put in your bank account number and IFSC code. This information must be the same
as the details provided in your GSTIN. Filling in this data ensures a prompt and secure payment cycle.
It is a transparent process where the seller receives their money in a safe and timely manner.

Create a New Listing

The final step of this online selling guide is to create a new listing of one of your products. It is one of
the most crucial stages of registration. It can factor into the buying decision of the shoppers.

In order to create an effective listing, you must add an accurate description and use high-definition
images. You must mention all the major selling points of the product and care instructions wherever
necessary. Moreover, you should also add details about the return and replacement policy. Let us
learn how to do that.

Click on the “Go to Listing” button on the onboarding page. Here you will see an option called “List
your own products”. It will take you to a page where you can register your product for it to go live.
Simply add the required details, and you are good to go.

Now that you know how to start an online selling business, register now and start your e-commerce
journey with Flipkart Seller Hub!
