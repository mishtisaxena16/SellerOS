Difference between Local Packaging & Approved Packaging

There are two types: local and approved packaging. Most people tend to find local packaging to be
efficient and cost-effective. However, one drawback is that it often does not offer the protection
required during transit. It’s like using a paper bag in the rain. This means your products can end up
damaged way before they even reach your customers.

Authorised or approved packaging, on the other hand, is specifically designed to meet Flipkart’s
standards. The main motive is to ensure your products stay safe on their journey. Sure, it might cost
more, but you can reduce product returns, and there would be no unhappy customers. In the end,
it’s an investment in your business’s reputation and in building trust with your buyers.
