What is Product Categorisation?

Product categorisation refers to the process of classifying and organising products into distinct
categories based on shared characteristics, attributes, or functionalities. It is fundamental to
managing an inventory or product catalogue when selling on an e-commerce platform. By placing
products into relevant categories, sellers can enhance discoverability, streamline the shopping
experience and efficiently manage their offerings. This ultimately drives more sales conversations for
the business.

For instance, if a business owner is selling electronics online, product categorisation would help them
group listings. Gadgets such as laptops, smartphones, cameras and others can be classified into
different categories and subcategories. Each e-commerce product classification has tags attached to
them called labels, helping customers find products that fit their requirements. This way, buyers can
navigate swiftly and effectively, especially when there are diverse and extensive product offerings on
the platform.

Why is E-commerce Product Categorisation Important?

Enhances Consumer Satisfaction

Proper product categorisation helps users quickly find the products they’re looking for, reducing the

leads to increased consumer satisfaction, encouraging repeat business and positive word-of-mouth

can ultimately lead to higher sales and growth.

Enables Category-Specific Features

Well-structured product categorisation enables sellers to implement category-specific features, such
as customised product recommendations, special deals and promotions. These tailored offers can be
targeted towards specific customer segments, increasing the likelihood of conversion. For instance, if
you have listed your products under a separate category for 'sale' items, you can promote them more
effectively and attract price-conscious buyers. Category-specific features help create a more
personalised shopping experience, fostering customer loyalty and driving recurring purchases.

Makes Searches More Relevant

A well-designed product categorisation system fosters better search results that are both accurate
and relevant. When customers enter keywords or apply filters, they receive a list of matching
products that meet their criteria. This relevancy improves the chances of completing a sale. Bundling
similar items together also helps in suggesting alternative products that might interest the customer,
further enhancing their shopping experience. This not only saves time for customers but also

Helps Customers Find The Right Products

Effective product categorisation empowers customers to narrow down their search by selecting
specific attributes, such as price range, brand, colour, size or any other specific features of the item.
This filtering process helps shoppers quickly eliminate unsuitable options and zero in on the most
suitable products. As a result, customers are more likely to find the right products, leading to
increased sales and reduced returns. When customers can easily check out and browse the catalogue
based on their search filters, they’re less likely to contact customer support or return items, saving
you time and resources.

E-commerce Guide: How To Categorise Products?

Know Your Product

The first step in effective product categorization is to know the product you’re selling. Understand its
features, benefits, and any unique selling points. This knowledge will help you place the product in
the correct category, making it easier for customers to find. Additionally, knowing your product well
allows you to write accurate product descriptions, which can further aid in effective categorisation.

Be Accurate and Detailed

When creating categories and subcategories, accuracy and detail are key. Use specific and descriptive
names for each, and avoid using overly broad or vague terms. For example, instead of categorising
men’s shirts in the 'clothing' category, use 'men’s clothing' and similarly for 'women’s clothing' and
'children’s clothing'.

Analyse Your Customer Base

Understanding your target audience is essential in creating effective product categorisation. This is
possible by analysing your customer base to determine what types of products they’re interested in
and how they typically search for them. This information can help you categorise according to their
preferences and behaviours. For instance, if your customer base primarily consists of professionals
searching for formal wear, you may want to use tags like 'business attire', 'office wear', or similar
terms to reach your audience.

What is Product Visibility?

Product visibility refers to how prominently and frequently your products are displayed in online
search results and marketplaces. It's about ensuring that when potential customers search for
products, yours ranks at the top of their search results, catching their eye and piquing their interest.
Similar to how physical stores attract customers with eye-catching displays, online product visibility is
about making your products the first and most appealing choice for online shoppers. Thus,
leveraging various digital tools and strategies to ensure your products stand out in the crowded
online marketplace is key to that.
