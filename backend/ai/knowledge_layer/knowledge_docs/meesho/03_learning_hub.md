# Meesho Seller Learning Hub

## Overview

The Meesho Learning Hub provides guidance on seller registration, catalog management, product compliance, inventory management, order processing, pricing, payments, business growth, and seller best practices.

---

# Seller Registration

## Registration Process

To start selling on Meesho:

1. Register as a seller.
2. Verify business information.
3. Add bank account details.
4. Set up pickup address.
5. Upload product catalogs.
6. Start receiving orders.

## GST Requirements

To sell on Meesho, sellers generally require:

- GSTIN
- Bank account
- Mobile number
- Email address

Eligible sellers without GST may register using applicable enrollment or identification mechanisms according to platform policies.

---

# Product Policies

## Prohibited Products

The following categories are not allowed on the platform:

- Alcohol and related products
- Tobacco products
- Illegal or restricted items
- Weapons and explosives
- Counterfeit products
- Hazardous materials
- Any product prohibited by law

Always verify platform policies before listing products.

---

# Legal Metrology Compliance

Products must comply with applicable labeling requirements.

## Required Information

Where applicable, product listings and packaging should include:

- Product name
- Manufacturer name and address
- Country of origin
- Net quantity
- Weight or volume
- MRP
- Dimensions
- Expiry date (if applicable)

Failure to comply may result in listing issues or penalties.

# Catalog Management

## Catalog Upload Methods

Meesho supports:

1. Single Catalog Upload
2. Bulk Catalog Upload

---

## Single Catalog Upload

Typical process:

1. Select category.
2. Upload product images.
3. Add product details.
4. Enter pricing.
5. Submit for review.

---

## Bulk Catalog Upload

### Workflow

1. Visit Catalog Upload.
2. Select Add Bulk Catalog.
3. Choose the correct category.
4. Download the category template.
5. Fill required details.
6. Upload the completed file.
7. Submit for review.

### Template Guidelines

- Red columns are mandatory.
- Green columns are recommended.
- Use dropdown values where available.
- Avoid unnecessarily long descriptions.
- Verify variation details before submission.

### Product Images

Images can be uploaded through the Images Bulk Upload section.

Use the generated image links inside the template before submission.

---

## Brand Registration

If products belong to a registered brand:

1. Raise a support request.
2. Submit brand proof documents.
3. Provide category details.
4. Submit the brand name exactly as it should appear.

After approval, the brand becomes available during catalog uploads.

---

## Quality Check (QC) Errors

Catalogs must pass quality checks before going live.

### Common QC Errors

- Wrong Category Error
- Duplicate SKU ID Error
- Duplicate Product ID Error
- SKU ID Error
- Image Link Error
- Product Description Error
- Image Upload Error

### Resolving QC Errors

1. Download the QC error file.
2. Review error messages.
3. Correct issues in the original file.
4. Upload the corrected file.
5. Wait for QC approval.

# Inventory Management

Inventory is managed through the Inventory section of the Supplier Panel.

## Inventory Status Types

### Active

Live catalogs currently available for sale.

### Activation Pending

Catalogs that passed QC but are not live yet.

### Blocked

Catalogs restricted from selling.

### Paused

Products temporarily unavailable for sale.

---

## Updating Inventory

### Single Product Update

1. Open inventory.
2. Click Edit.
3. Update stock quantity.
4. Save changes.

### Bulk Stock Update

1. Download inventory sheet.
2. Update stock quantities.
3. Upload the updated file.

---

## Product Variations

New variants can be added by:

1. Selecting Add New Variation.
2. Entering variation details.
3. Updating SKU ID.
4. Updating stock count.

---

## Inventory Best Practices

- Maintain accurate stock counts.
- Update inventory regularly.
- Mark unavailable products as out of stock.
- Avoid overselling products.

Recommended stock level:

- Maintain at least 20 units for live products where possible.

---

## Smart Inventory Suggestions

Inventory suggestions provide:

- Estimated future demand
- Expected inventory depletion dates

Use these insights to plan restocking activities.

# Order Management

## Seller Workflow

Typical seller journey:

Register
→ Upload Catalog
→ Receive Orders
→ Process Orders
→ Ship Orders
→ Receive Payments

---

## Processing Orders

### Step 1

Check new orders in the Pending section.

### Step 2

Accept orders and begin packaging.

Accepted orders move to Ready to Ship.

### Step 3

Package products securely.

### Step 4

Download and attach shipping labels.

### Step 5

Hand over packages to logistics partners.

Orders then move to Shipped status.

---

# Packaging Guidelines

## Packaging Requirements

Packaging should:

- Be durable
- Protect products during transit
- Avoid transparent packaging
- Avoid seller branding
- Avoid branded adhesive tape

Do not print MRP information on outer packaging.

---

## Packing Efficiency

Use compact packaging where possible.

Fragile products should receive additional protection.

---

# Shipping and Dispatch

## Next-Day Dispatch (NDD)

Next-Day Dispatch requires orders to be dispatched the day after receipt.

Benefits include:

- Improved seller performance
- Better customer satisfaction
- Increased sales opportunities

---

## Shipping Labels

For same-day pickup:

- Download shipping labels before 11 AM.

Labels downloaded after 11 AM may be picked up on the next day.

---

# Returns and RTO

## Return to Origin (RTO)

If a customer cannot be reached after multiple delivery attempts, the shipment is returned to the seller.

These orders appear in the Return/RTO section.

---

## Customer Returns

When customers initiate returns:

- Products are returned through the logistics network.
- Return shipping fees may apply.

Check product-level return fee details where applicable.

---

## Hold Orders

Orders may move to Hold status when delivery becomes temporarily unavailable due to logistics constraints.

---

# Common Seller Mistakes

## Ignoring Dispatch Deadlines

Late dispatches increase cancellation risk and negatively impact seller performance.

Use the "Breaching Soon" filter to identify urgent orders.

---

## Late Shipping Label Downloads

For same-day pickups, download labels before 11 AM.

---

## Poor Pickup Documentation

Maintain records of:

- Pickup date
- Pickup agent
- Quantity shipped
- Delivery partner confirmation

---

## Incorrect Inventory Counts

Avoid listing stock that is not available.

Regularly verify inventory levels.

---

## Not Marking Products Out of Stock

Immediately update inventory when stock becomes unavailable.

This prevents avoidable order cancellations.
