STEP 10 : PRODUCT PACKING

GUIDELINES

11

 Products should be packed in pure white polyester packets or cartons (if the product

is fragile)

 Since Resellers are selling your products to THEIR customers at a different price,

your branding and MRP tag must not be visible anywhere on the product or
packaging

 Never use a packaging material that reflects your MRP

 Properly package the product in order to avoid RTOs, returns and poor ratings

 Regarding Plastic Ban :  It is recommended you use an alternative packaging material

like Corrugated Sheets, Threaded Paper Bags, Water resistive high GSM Paper
envelopes and any other alternative. Meesho will not be liable for any detention and
penalties/ legal implications, in case of any shipment with plastic handed over to
logistics providers by you, which shall be at your own risk and consequences.
