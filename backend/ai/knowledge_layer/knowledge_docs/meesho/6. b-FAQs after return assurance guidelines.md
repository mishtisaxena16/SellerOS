# Return Assurance Program (RAP) - Frequently Asked Questions

## Overview

This document answers common questions about Meesho's Return Assurance Program (RAP), a subscription-based service that compensates sellers for eligible return shipping costs on returned or exchanged orders.

---

## What is the Return Assurance Program (RAP)?

The Return Assurance Program (RAP) is a seller protection service that reimburses eligible return shipping costs for returned or exchanged orders. It helps reduce the financial impact of unexpected increases in returns.

---

## How can I view my current RAP plan?

You can view your:

- Active plan status
- Coverage details
- Next payment date

by opening the **Service Plan Details** popup in the supplier portal dashboard.

---

## Who is eligible for RAP?

A supplier is eligible if they have:

- At least **6 months of return history**
- A minimum of **10 orders per month** for **three consecutive months**

---

## What are cohorts?

Suppliers are grouped into risk cohorts based on the consistency of their return rates over the previous six months.

The grouping uses the standard deviation of historical return rates.

| Cohort | Percentile | Description |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Cohort 1 | 0–20% | Most consistent suppliers with the lowest return fluctuations |
| Cohort 2 | 21–40% | Low variation in return rates |
| Cohort 3 | 41–60% | Average market fluctuation |
| Cohort 4 | 61–80% | Higher variation in return rates |
| Cohort 5 | 81–100% | Highest return fluctuations |

---

## Can my cohort change?

No.

Once enrolled, your cohort assignment and pricing remain fixed for the duration of the active enrollment period, providing predictable costs.

---

## How is Return Percentage Coverage calculated?

The program compares:

- Previous month's return rate (baseline)
- Current month's return rate

Coverage is based on the increase ("spike") in return percentage.

### Small Spike

If the increase is less than **half of the baseline**, the entire increase is covered.

### Large Spike

If the increase exceeds half of the baseline, coverage is capped at **half of the baseline percentage**.

---

## How is compensation calculated?

For every eligible returned or exchanged order:

- Return shipping fees are reimbursed.
- Maximum compensation is **₹130 per order**.
- Compensation equals the actual shipping cost or ₹130, whichever is lower.

Eligible orders are selected in ascending order of shipping cost until the calculated coverage limit is reached.

---

## Example

Suppose:

- Baseline return rate = **10%**
- Current return rate = **14%**
- Total orders = **1,000**

The return spike is **4%**.

Since 4% is less than half of the 10% baseline (5%), RAP covers the full 4% spike.

In this case, return shipping costs for **40 eligible returned or exchanged orders** are reimbursed.

---

## How much does RAP cost?

The program fee is charged daily as a percentage of Daily Sales Revenue.

| Cohort | Daily Fee |
|---------|----------:|
| Cohort 1 | 0.9% |
| Cohort 2 | 1.0% |
| Cohort 3 | 1.2% |
| Cohort 4 | 1.5% |
| Cohort 5 | 1.7% |

Daily Sales Revenue is calculated as:

> (Price × Quantity) − Return Shipping Fees

The percentage is inclusive of GST.

---

## When are payouts made?

Compensation is processed automatically.

Because orders placed on the last day of a month remain eligible for returns for seven days, payouts are processed on the **8th day of the following month**.

No manual claim submission is required.

---

## When is the RAP fee deducted?

Program fees are deducted daily based on:

- Assigned cohort
- Daily Sales Revenue

---

## What happens if I cancel the program?

You may cancel RAP at any time using the **Cancel Service** option in the supplier portal.

Coverage remains active until the end of the current billing cycle.

Example:

- Opt out on January 20
- Coverage continues until January 31
- No further deductions from February 1

---

## What happens if I have outstanding recoverable amounts?

Outstanding recoverable amounts are collected from the supplier ledger according to Meesho's reconciliation policy.

---

## What are the benefits of RAP?

Benefits include:

- Protection against sudden increases in return costs
- Predictable operational expenses
- Automatic compensation processing
- Reduced financial risk
- Better business planning and budgeting

---

## Where can I see my cohort and pricing?

Open the **Service Plan Details** popup in the supplier portal.

The **Plan Amount** section displays:

- Assigned cohort
- Daily percentage rate

---

## What happens if I have zero sales?

If Daily Sales Revenue is ₹0:

- Daily RAP fee is ₹0.
- Coverage remains active.

---

## What should I do if I believe my compensation is incorrect?

You can raise a dispute through the **Supplier Portal Support** section.

The support team will review the calculation based on your assigned cohort and applicable coverage rules.
