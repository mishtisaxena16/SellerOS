# Amazon Seller Onboarding Guide

# Overview

Amazon Seller University provides official learning resources that help sellers register, list products, manage inventory, fulfill orders, and grow their business.

---

# Registration Requirements

Before selling on Amazon, sellers should prepare:
- PAN Card
- GST Registration (where applicable)
- Bank Account
- Mobile Number
- Email Address

Business details should remain consistent across all documents.

---

# Business Types

Amazon supports various business structures including:
- Sole Proprietorship
- Partnership
- LLP
- Private Limited Company

Choose the structure appropriate for your business.

---

# Tax Compliance

Marketplace sellers should comply with GST requirements.

Key responsibilities include:
- GST registration
- Filing GST returns
- Maintaining tax invoices
- Monitoring TCS deductions

---

# Creating Product Listings

Products can be listed using:
- Individual listings
- Bulk uploads
- Existing ASINs

Ensure:
- Accurate product information
- Correct category selection
- Proper variation relationships
- High-quality product images

---

# Fulfillment Options

Amazon supports multiple fulfillment methods.

## Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)

Amazon stores, packs, ships, and manages customer service.

## Easy Ship

Seller stores inventory while Amazon collects and delivers orders.

## Self Ship

Seller manages storage, packing, and delivery independently.

---

# FAQs

### Is GST registration required?

Marketplace sellers generally require GST registration based on applicable regulations.

### Which fulfillment method is best?

It depends on inventory management, logistics capability, and business goals.

### Can I edit product listings later?

Yes. Product information can be updated whenever necessary.
