# Amazon Seller Policies and Code of Conduct

# Overview
Amazon requires every seller to follow marketplace policies that ensure a safe, transparent, and trustworthy shopping experience. Violating these policies can result in listing removal, account suspension, or permanent account deactivation.

---

# Business Requirements

Sellers must:
- Register using their legal business name.
- Ensure the registered business name matches the PAN and GST registration.
- Maintain accurate business information.
- List products only in the correct product category.

Incorrect categorization may lead to listing suppression or policy violations.

---

# Seller Account Policy

Amazon generally permits one Seller Central account per marketplace.

A second account is allowed only with valid business justification, such as:
- Operating separate registered brands
- Managing independent businesses

Linked accounts are monitored. If one linked account violates Amazon policies, associated accounts may also be affected.

---

# Product Listing Guidelines

When creating listings, sellers should:
- Provide accurate product titles and descriptions.
- Use genuine product images.
- Select the correct product category.
- Avoid misleading information.
- Keep inventory information updated.

---

# Review and Feedback Policy

Amazon strictly prohibits review manipulation.

The following activities are not allowed:
- Paying customers for reviews
- Offering gifts or discounts in exchange for reviews
- Requesting only positive reviews
- Asking customers to remove negative reviews
- Reviewing your own products
- Asking employees or family members to submit reviews
- Posting fake negative reviews on competitors' listings

Only authentic customer feedback is permitted.

---

# Marketplace Integrity

Sellers must never attempt to manipulate Amazon's marketplace.

Prohibited activities include:
- Artificially increasing product traffic
- Using bots or automated clicks
- Creating fake orders
- Manipulating Best Seller Rank
- Damaging competitor listings

Violations may lead to immediate account suspension.

---

# Seller Fees (2026 Update)

Effective March 16, 2026:

## Referral Fees

- Products priced up to ₹1,000 have zero referral fees across many eligible categories.
- Referral fees for products above ₹1,000 have been reduced for selected categories.

## Closing Fees

For Self-Ship sellers:

- Lower closing fees apply to products below ₹500.

## GST on Amazon Fees

Amazon charges 18% GST on marketplace service fees, including:
- Referral fees
- Closing fees
- Shipping-related fees

# Best Practices
- Keep business documents updated.
- Follow category-specific policies.
- Maintain accurate product information.
- Monitor account health regularly.
- Respond promptly to policy notifications.

---

# Key Takeaways
- One seller account per marketplace is the standard policy.
- Fake reviews and marketplace manipulation are prohibited.
- Accurate listings improve compliance.
- Business information should always match official registrations.
- Follow fee updates and marketplace policies regularly.

---

# FAQs

### Can I have multiple Amazon seller accounts?
Only with a valid business reason and Amazon's approval.

### Can I offer discounts for reviews?
No. Incentivizing reviews violates Amazon policy.

### What happens if I violate Amazon policies?
Violations may result in listing removal, account suspension, withheld payments, or permanent account deactivation.
