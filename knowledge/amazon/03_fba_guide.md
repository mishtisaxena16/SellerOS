# Amazon FBA and Fulfillment Guide

# Overview

Amazon provides multiple fulfillment options that allow sellers to choose how products are stored, packed, shipped, and returned. The choice of fulfillment model affects delivery speed, seller responsibilities, customer experience, and operational costs.

---

# Fulfillment Options

## Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)

With Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), sellers send inventory to Amazon Fulfillment Centers. Amazon manages storage, order processing, packing, shipping, customer service, and returns.

### Benefits
- Prime eligibility for eligible products
- Faster deliveries
- Amazon-managed customer service
- Simplified returns handling
- Reduced operational workload for sellers

FBA is suitable for businesses looking to scale operations while outsourcing logistics.

---

## Easy Ship
Easy Ship is a hybrid fulfillment model where the seller stores inventory and packs customer orders, while Amazon collects the shipment and delivers it to the customer.

### Benefits
- Amazon-managed last-mile delivery
- Seller retains inventory control
- Lower infrastructure requirements than FBA

This model is popular among sellers who maintain their own warehouse but prefer Amazon's delivery network.

---

## Self Ship (Merchant Fulfilled Network)

Under Self Ship, sellers manage the complete fulfillment process independently.

Seller responsibilities include:
- Inventory storage
- Packing orders
- Selecting courier partners
- Shipping products
- Tracking deliveries
- Managing customer returns

This option offers greater operational control but requires strong logistics management.

---

# Logistics Charges

Amazon logistics charges vary based on:
- Fulfillment method
- Product weight
- Package dimensions
- Delivery destination
- Shipping distance

Fees are updated periodically according to Amazon's pricing policies.

---

# Weight Calculation

Shipping charges are calculated using either:
- Actual weight
- Volumetric weight

The higher value is generally used for fee calculation.

### Volumetric Weight Formula

Volumetric Weight (kg) = (Length × Breadth × Height in cm) ÷ 5000
Accurate package dimensions help avoid unexpected shipping charges.

---

# FBA Storage

FBA inventory is stored in Amazon Fulfillment Centers.

Storage fees depend on factors such as:
- Inventory volume
- Storage duration
- Seasonal demand

Additional charges may apply for inventory stored beyond the permitted duration.

---

# Order Processing

For FBA orders, Amazon handles:
- Picking products
- Packing orders
- Shipping
- Customer service
- Returns processing

Processing charges vary depending on product size and category.

---

# Delivery Performance

Sellers using Easy Ship or Self Ship should maintain reliable order fulfillment performance.

Key operational practices include:
- Dispatch orders on time
- Maintain accurate inventory
- Provide valid tracking information
- Minimize order cancellations
- Deliver within committed timelines

Consistent delivery performance contributes to better account health and customer satisfaction.

---

# Best Practices

- Choose the fulfillment model based on business size and operational capability.
- Measure package dimensions accurately.
- Monitor storage costs regularly.
- Avoid long-term inventory accumulation.
- Track logistics performance and delivery metrics.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Amazon offers three fulfillment options: FBA, Easy Ship, and Self Ship.
- FBA provides end-to-end logistics management by Amazon.
- Easy Ship combines seller-managed inventory with Amazon delivery.
- Self Ship gives sellers full control over fulfillment operations.
- Accurate weight and dimension data help optimize shipping costs.

# FAQs

### Which fulfillment option is best for new sellers?
The choice depends on inventory volume, logistics capability, and business goals.

### Does FBA include customer service?
Yes. Amazon manages customer service and returns for FBA orders.

### How are shipping charges calculated?
Shipping fees are generally based on whichever is higher between actual weight and volumetric weight.

### Can sellers switch between fulfillment methods?
Yes. Sellers can choose different fulfillment methods for different products based on operational needs.
