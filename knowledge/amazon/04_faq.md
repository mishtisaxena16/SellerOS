# Amazon Seller FAQs

# Overview

This document answers common questions related to selling on Amazon India.

---

# Registration

## Is GST registration required?
Marketplace sellers generally require GST registration before selling.

---

## What documents are needed?

Typical documents include:
- PAN Card
- GST Registration
- Bank Account
- Mobile Number
- Email Address

---

# Listings

## What is the Buy Box?

The Buy Box is the primary purchase section on a product page.

Eligibility depends on factors such as:
- Competitive pricing
- Seller performance
- Inventory availability
- Fulfillment quality

---

## How can I improve Buy Box eligibility?

Focus on:
- Competitive pricing
- Fast fulfillment
- Good customer service
- Strong account health

---

# Returns

## How are returns handled?

For FBA orders: Amazon manages customer communication and return processing.

For Self Ship: The seller manages return logistics according to Amazon policies.

---

# Compliance

## What are Legal Metrology requirements?

Certain packaged goods must display mandatory declarations such as:
- MRP
- Net quantity
- Manufacturer or importer information
- Country of Origin

---

## Why is Country of Origin important?

Imported products should include accurate Country of Origin information to comply with applicable regulations.

---

# Fees

## What are referral fees?

Referral fees are commissions charged by Amazon for each sale and vary by product category.

---

## Does Amazon charge GST on fees?

Yes.
Marketplace service fees generally attract GST.

---

# Best Practices

- Read Amazon policies regularly.
- Keep compliance documents updated.
- Monitor account health.
- Maintain accurate product information.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Compliance is essential for uninterrupted selling.
- Accurate listings improve visibility.
- Good seller performance increases Buy Box eligibility.
- Stay updated with fee and policy changes.
