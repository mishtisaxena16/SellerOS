# Amazon Listing Requirements

## Title Requirements

### 75-Character Title Limit

Amazon requires product titles in most categories to be **75 characters or fewer**, including spaces.

Exceptions may apply to media categories such as books and DVDs.

### Automated Title Updates

- Titles exceeding the limit may be automatically rewritten by Amazon.
- Brand-registered sellers typically receive a review period before automated changes are published.
- Keep titles concise and focused on key product information.

---

## Item Highlights

Amazon provides a dedicated **Item Highlights** field for additional product information.

### Purpose

Use this section to include:

- Material information
- Product dimensions
- Primary use cases
- Key technical specifications
- Important product attributes

### Benefits

- Improves product clarity
- Enhances customer experience
- Remains searchable within Amazon's catalog system
- Supports both desktop and mobile shopping experiences

---

## Title Formatting Guidelines

### Capitalization

- Capitalize major words.
- Do not use ALL CAPS.
- Minor words such as:
  - in
  - the
  - and
  - with

may remain lowercase when appropriate.

### Numbers

Use numerical values instead of written words.

**Correct Examples**

- 2 Pack
- 5 Pieces
- 10 cm

**Avoid**

- Two Pack
- Five Pieces
- Ten cm

---

## Backend Search Terms

Backend search terms help Amazon index products and improve discoverability.

### Search Term Limit

- Maximum: 250 bytes
- Bytes may differ from character count, especially for multilingual content.

### Best Practices

Include:

- Relevant product keywords
- Alternate search phrases
- Regional terminology
- Common customer search variations

Example:

- shoes
- sneakers
- footwear
- joota

### Avoid Including

- Brand names (yours or competitors')
- ASINs
- Promotional terms such as:
  - Best
  - Cheapest
  - Number 1
- Duplicate keywords already present in the title

### Formatting Rules

- Separate keywords using single spaces.
- Do not use:
  - Commas
  - Semicolons
  - Quotation marks
  - Special characters unless necessary

---

## Seller Recommendations

### Title Optimization

- Keep titles within Amazon limits.
- Prioritize important keywords at the beginning.
- Maintain readability for customers.

### Item Highlights Usage

Use Item Highlights to capture:

- Features
- Dimensions
- Materials
- Usage information

instead of overloading the product title.

### Keyword Strategy

- Use relevant backend search terms.
- Include regional and local language variations where appropriate.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and repetition.

---

## Summary

To maintain compliant and high-performing Amazon listings:

1. Keep titles under 75 characters.
2. Follow capitalization and numbering guidelines.
3. Use Item Highlights for detailed product information.
4. Optimize backend search terms within the 250-byte limit.
5. Avoid prohibited keyword practices and duplicate content.
