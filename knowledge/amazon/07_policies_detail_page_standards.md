# Amazon Policies & Detail Page Standards

## Detail Page Integrity

Each Amazon product detail page must represent only one unique product.

### Requirements

- One detail page = one product.
- Product information must accurately match the item being sold.
- Reviews and ratings must belong to the same product represented on the page.

### Not Allowed

- Replacing an existing product with a different product.
- Using a high-rated ASIN to sell unrelated items.
- Modifying a listing in a way that misleads customers.

---

## Product Updates and New ASIN Requirements

When a product undergoes significant changes, a new ASIN may be required.

### Examples of Significant Changes

- Material changes
- Design changes
- Feature changes
- Performance upgrades
- Product name changes
- New model releases

Example:

- Mixer Grinder 500W → Mixer Grinder 750W

### Guidelines

- Create a new listing when the product is substantially different.
- Maintain historical listings for previous product versions.
- Ensure product information accurately reflects the version being sold.

### Risks of Incorrect Updates

- Listing suppression
- Catalog compliance issues
- Reduced product visibility
- Loss of ranking and sales performance

---

## Restricted Content on Detail Pages

Amazon restricts certain content across all listing fields, including:

- Product titles
- Bullet points
- Descriptions
- Images
- A+ Content

### Promotional Language

Avoid promotional phrases such as:

- Special Offer
- Clearance Sale
- Free Gift
- Limited Time Promotion

### Subjective Claims

Avoid unverifiable claims such as:

- Best Seller
- #1 Rated
- Top Seller
- Extra Value
- Most Popular

### Contact Information

Do not include:

- Phone numbers
- WhatsApp numbers
- Email addresses
- Website URLs
- Physical store addresses

### Code and Script Restrictions

Do not include:

- JavaScript
- Executable code
- Unsupported HTML

Only basic formatting supported by Amazon should be used.

---

## Language and Translation Standards

Listings may be adapted for different languages and regions.

### Requirements

- Information must remain accurate across all language versions.
- Product specifications must match in every translation.
- Measurements and numerical values must remain consistent.
- Product descriptions should use clear and understandable language.

### Best Practices

- Use simple wording.
- Avoid ambiguous translations.
- Verify translated dimensions, weights, and specifications.

---

## Consumer Protection Compliance

Sellers must comply with applicable consumer protection regulations.

### Pricing Accuracy

- Display accurate pricing information.
- Ensure MRP and selling price are correctly represented.
- Avoid misleading price claims.

### Seller Transparency

Required information should remain visible where applicable, including:

- Seller details
- Product information
- Pricing information
- Applicable disclosures

---

## Seller Recommendations

Before publishing a listing:

1. Verify that the product matches the ASIN.
2. Create a new listing when major product changes occur.
3. Remove promotional or unverifiable claims.
4. Avoid sharing contact information on detail pages.
5. Ensure translations remain accurate.
6. Verify pricing and product information for compliance.

---

## Summary

To maintain compliant Amazon listings:

- Use one detail page per product.
- Create new ASINs for major product changes.
- Avoid promotional, misleading, or subjective claims.
- Do not include contact information or unsupported code.
- Maintain accurate translations and pricing information.
- Follow Amazon catalog and consumer protection requirements.
