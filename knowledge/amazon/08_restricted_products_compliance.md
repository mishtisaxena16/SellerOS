# Amazon Restricted Products & Compliance Requirements

## Legal Metrology (LMPC) Compliance

### Overview

Imported packaged products sold through e-commerce platforms must comply with applicable Legal Metrology requirements.

### Required Product Declarations

Packaged products should clearly display:

- Country of Origin
- Net Quantity
- Manufacturer details
- Importer details (if applicable)
- Month of Manufacture
- Year of Manufacture
- Maximum Retail Price (MRP)
- Applicable taxes and pricing information

### Country of Origin Requirements

Imported and domestically manufactured products should be clearly distinguished.

Product information should allow customers to identify:

- Country of Origin
- Imported products
- Domestic products

### Compliance Risks

Failure to comply may result in:

- Product listing removal
- Regulatory penalties
- Product seizure
- Legal action by authorities

---

## FSSAI Compliance for Food Products

### Licensing Requirements

Food and beverage products require a valid Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) license or registration before being sold online.

### License Categories

#### Basic Registration

Applicable for businesses with smaller turnover limits.

#### State License

Applicable for medium-scale food businesses.

#### Central License

Applicable for large-scale food businesses and businesses operating under categories requiring central approval.

### Seller Responsibilities

Ensure:

- Valid FSSAI registration or license
- Accurate food labeling
- Compliance with food safety regulations
- Updated license information

### Non-Compliance Risks

Failure to comply may result in:

- Listing suspension
- Product removal
- Regulatory penalties
- Account compliance actions

---

## BIS Certification Requirements

### Products Commonly Requiring BIS Certification

Examples include:

- Electronics
- Toys
- Helmets
- Other regulated product categories

### Seller Responsibilities

Before listing products:

- Verify BIS certification requirements.
- Obtain necessary approvals.
- Ensure products carry required certification markings.
- Maintain supporting compliance documents.

### Risks of Non-Compliance

- Listing removal
- Compliance violations
- Regulatory penalties
- Restricted selling privileges

---

## Hazardous Goods Requirements

Certain products may require additional documentation before they can be sold or shipped.

### Examples

- Lithium batteries
- Flammable liquids
- Chemical products
- Other regulated dangerous goods

### Seller Requirements

Provide:

- Safety documentation
- Product specifications
- Required regulatory certifications
- Shipping compliance information

Failure to provide required documentation may result in listing restrictions or account actions.

---

## Restricted and Prohibited Products

Some products may be restricted or prohibited from sale.

Examples can include:

- Products prohibited by law
- Uncertified regulated products
- Restricted children's products
- Unsafe consumer products

Sellers should verify category-specific requirements before listing any regulated item.

---

## Seller Compliance Checklist

Before listing a product:

### Product Information

- Verify Country of Origin details.
- Confirm manufacturer and importer information.
- Ensure MRP and quantity declarations are accurate.

### Food Products

- Obtain valid FSSAI registration or license.
- Verify food labeling compliance.

### Regulated Products

- Check BIS certification requirements.
- Maintain compliance documentation.

### Hazardous Goods

- Prepare safety documentation.
- Verify shipping eligibility and restrictions.

### Restricted Products

- Confirm the product is permitted for sale.
- Review marketplace policies before listing.

---

## Summary

To maintain compliant Amazon listings:

1. Provide all required Legal Metrology declarations.
2. Clearly identify Country of Origin information.
3. Obtain required FSSAI approvals for food products.
4. Ensure BIS certification for regulated categories.
5. Maintain documentation for hazardous goods.
6. Verify that products are not restricted or prohibited before listing.
