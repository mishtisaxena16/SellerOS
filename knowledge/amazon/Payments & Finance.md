# **Amazon Seller Payments & Finance Knowledge Base**

This knowledge base explains how Amazon manages seller payments, settlements, selling fees, taxes, reimbursements, refunds, financial reports, and payment-related compliance. Understanding these processes helps sellers accurately predict payouts, manage cash flow, reduce unnecessary fees, and remain compliant with Amazon policies and Indian tax regulations.

## **Amazon Payment System and Settlement Process**

Amazon utilizes a structured settlement process to manage sales proceeds, fees, and disbursements to sellers.

### **Settlement Amount Calculation**

The **Settlement Amount** remitted to a seller is calculated as the gross **Sales Proceeds** minus several deductions. These deductions include:

* **Referral Fees** due for the transactions.
* **Selling on Amazon Subscription Fees**.
* **Taxes** required to be charged by Amazon on its service fees.
* **Refunds** due to customers.
* **Reserves** held by Amazon.
* **Closing Fees**, if applicable.
* Any other applicable fees prescribed under Program Policies, such as **FBA Fees**.
* **Tax Collected at Source (TCS)** under applicable Law.

### **Payment Cycle and Disbursements**

* **Standard Cycle:** For many sellers, Amazon collects payments from customers and pays out available funds every two weeks.
* **Settlement Timing:** Payments for **Eligible Transactions** (those where the actual shipment date has been confirmed) are generally received within 5-7 business days of the payment being generated through Automated Clearing House (ACH) or electronic transfer.
* **STEP Program Impact:** Sellers in higher tiers of the **STEP program** (a performance-based benefits program) may be eligible for faster disbursement cycles.
* **Initial/Updated Bank Details:** When a seller initially provides or changes their bank account information, the Payment Date is deferred for up to 14 calendar days.
* **Payment Method:** Remittances for non-COD transactions are made through a **Nodal Account** in accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directions. COD remittances are made through online banking or other mutually agreed means to the seller's bank account.

### **Payment Holds and Reserves**

* **Risk-Based Withholding:** Amazon may withhold payments if it determines a seller's actions or performance may result in returns, chargebacks, claims, or disputes.
* **Reserve Fund:** Amazon may hold back a portion of sales proceeds as a separate **Reserve**. This fund is used to settle future customer claims, refunds, and chargebacks.
* **Policy Violations:** Payments may be permanently withheld if an account is used for deceptive, fraudulent, or illegal activity, such as selling counterfeit goods.

### **Common Reasons for Payment Holds**

- Recently changed bank account information.
- High refund or chargeback rate.
- Poor account health.
- Policy violations.
- Suspicious or fraudulent account activity.
- Pending identity verification.

## **Selling Fees and Charges**

Sellers incur various fees depending on their selling plan, product category, and fulfillment method.

### **Standard Selling Fees**

* **Subscription Fees:** These are based on the selling plan. The Professional plan typically involves a monthly flat fee, while the Individual plan may involve a per-item fee.
* **Referral Fees:** A fee charged as a percentage of the total sales price (including item price and any shipping, gift wrap, or other charges). This percentage varies by product category.
 * For orders with **promotions**, the referral fee is calculated on the total sales price *after* the promotion has been applied.
* **Closing Fees:** A fee charged in addition to the referral fee, based on the product price.

### **Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Fees**

FBA fees cover the storage and delivery services provided by Amazon.

* **Fulfillment Fees:** Charged per unit sold; includes picking, packing, shipping, handling, customer service, and processing returns. Shipping fees for FBA can start as low as ₹28 per item.
* **Storage Fees:** Charged monthly based on the volume of inventory held in an Amazon Fulfillment Center (FC). These fees begin when a unit arrives and is available for fulfillment.
* **Removal and Disposal Fees:** Charged when a seller requests units be returned to them or destroyed.
* **Unplanned Prep Fees:** Incurred if units requiring labels are received at the FC with missing barcodes.

### **Amazon Easy Ship Fees**

* **Weight-Handling Fee:** Calculated based on a **weight slab**, which is determined by the higher of the actual (dead) weight or the volumetric weight. Fees start at ₹38 per item and vary by volume and delivery distance.
* **Audit Fees:** Providing incorrect weight and dimensions during pickup scheduling may result in an additional audit fee.

### **Best Selling Fee Practices**

- Understand all applicable fees before pricing products.
- Regularly review referral fee changes.
- Monitor storage fees to avoid unnecessary long-term costs.
- Compare fulfillment methods before listing products.
- Include Amazon fees while calculating product profit margins.

## **Returns, Refunds, and Reimbursements**

### **Refund Obligations and Charges**

* **Seller Responsibility:** Sellers must route all refund payments through Amazon.
* **Refund Administration Fee:** For every refund, Amazon may retain a fee, which is the lesser of INR 300 or 20% of the applicable Referral Fee.
* **Refund Commission Fee:** Charged when a customer returns a shipment.
* **Self-Ship Adjustments:** Sellers calculate refunds (including taxes and shipping) using Seller Central functionality, and Amazon debits the seller's account to pay the customer.

### **Reimbursements**

* **Undeliverable/Rejected Orders:** Referral and closing fees are automatically reimbursed for eligible orders that are refused by the customer or marked undeliverable.
* **FBA Lost/Damaged Inventory:** If units are lost or damaged while stored in an FC due to Amazon's fault, Amazon pays the seller the **replacement value**.
 * Title of the unit transfers to Amazon upon payment of the replacement value.
* **SAFE-T Claims:** Sellers can file a SAFE-T claim if they believe a refund was wrongly debited from their account for Easy Ship or Self-Ship orders (e.g., for "returnless" refunds they disagree with).
* **Fair Market Value:** Proactive reimbursements are often based on the product's fair market value, minus applicable taxes and Amazon fees.

### **Common Returns, Refunds, and Reimbursements Mistakes**

- Issuing refunds outside Seller Central.
- Ignoring SAFE-T claim eligibility.
- Delaying refund processing.
- Not reviewing reimbursement reports.
- Missing reimbursement deadlines.

## **Tax Compliance and Reporting**

### **Why Tax Compliance Matters**

Incorrect GST information, tax invoices, or Product Tax Codes can lead to compliance issues, delayed settlements, customer disputes, and penalties. Maintaining accurate financial records also simplifies audits and tax filing.

### **Seller Tax Responsibilities**

Sellers are solely responsible for the collection and payment of their own taxes, including GST/VAT, and for filing relevant returns.

* **Tax Invoices:** Sellers must issue valid tax invoices, credit notes, and debit notes where required.
* **Product Tax Codes:** It is the seller's responsibility to choose correct product tax codes and assign the proper HSN/SAC codes to ensure the correct tax rate is applied to listings.

### **Amazon’s Tax Roles (TDS, TCS, and GST on Fees)**

* **TCS/TDS:** Amazon is required by law to deduct and deposit **Tax Collection at Source (TCS)** and **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** from payments made to sellers.
* **GST on Fees:** All fees payable to Amazon are exclusive of GST. Sellers must pay any taxes imposed on these fees.
* **Withholding Tax Reimbursement:** If a seller deposits withholding tax as required by law, they can submit a reimbursement claim to Amazon with a valid Form 16A certificate.

### **Financial and Tax Reporting**

* **Merchant Tax Report (MTR):** The primary report available in Seller Central for reporting transactions.
* **B2B Reporting:** For B2B orders, sellers must file taxes at an **invoice level** (including the buyer's GSTIN) to allow business customers to claim Input Tax Credit. A consolidated B2B report is usually available by the 6th of each month.
* **E-Invoicing and E-Waybills:** Eligible FBA sellers must generate an **Invoice Reference Number (IRN)** through the government portal and share it with Amazon 24 hours before an interstate pickup. Failure to do so can result in cancelled pickups or suspended privileges.

## **Financial Management Best Practices**

- Reconcile settlements regularly with Seller Central reports.
- Download settlement reports after every payment cycle.
- Monitor fee deductions for unexpected charges.
- Track reimbursement claims until they are resolved.
- Maintain accurate bookkeeping for taxes and audits.
- Keep bank details updated.
- Review profit margins after every fee update.

## **Pay by Invoice (B2B)**

**Pay by Invoice** allows qualified Amazon Business customers to purchase products with a **30-day credit period**.

* **Seller Payments:** Amazon credits the seller's account when the buyer pays. If the buyer is late, Amazon guarantees payment to the seller by the **7th day past the due date**.
* **Credit Risk:** Amazon bears the risk and manages the creditworthiness assessment and collections.
* **Invoicing:** Amazon automatically generates the invoice for these orders with the necessary payment instructions for the business customer.

## **Bank Account and Financial Requirements**

* **India-Based Account:** Sellers must maintain a valid bank account in their name with a bank located within India.
* **Credit Card:** Amazon may require valid credit card information to charge for any sums payable by the seller that cannot be offset against sales proceeds.
* **Verification:** Sellers authorize Amazon to verify their information and obtain credit reports from time to time.

---

## **Important Financial Notes and Warnings**

* **Cancellation Charges:** Sellers are charged a cancellation fee (100% of referral fee if cancelled before ESD; 150% if after) for orders cancelled for reasons other than a buyer request, or if shipping confirmation is delayed.
* **Cashback Funding:** For cashback promotions, the seller funds the entire amount. Amazon initially applies it at purchase and then offsets it from the seller's balance.
* **Interest:** Sellers are not entitled to interest on balances maintained as deposits in Amazon's bank accounts.
* **Accuracy of Data:** Providing incorrect weight or dimensions for Easy Ship leads to slab changes and extra fees; image proof for corrections must be submitted within 15 days.

## **Common Payment Issues**

### **Payment Not Received**

Possible reasons include:

- Settlement cycle not completed.
- Bank account recently updated.
- Pending account verification.
- Payment reserve applied.
- Banking information is incorrect.

### **Incorrect Settlement Amount**

Possible reasons include:

- Referral fee deductions.
- Refund adjustments.
- Storage fees.
- FBA fees.
- TCS or TDS deductions.
- Reserve balance.

## **Seller Finance Checklist**

Before operating your Amazon business, ensure that:

- Bank account is verified.
- GST information is accurate.
- Product Tax Codes are correctly assigned.
- Settlement reports are reviewed regularly.
- Selling fees are understood.
- Refund reports are monitored.
- Reimbursement reports are checked.
- Financial records are maintained for tax filing.

> **Important**
>
> Amazon may modify selling fees, settlement schedules, tax requirements, and reimbursement policies over time. Sellers should regularly review Seller Central announcements and financial reports to ensure continued compliance and accurate financial planning.
