# **SellerOS Registration Knowledge Base: Amazon.in**

## **Seller Eligibility and Account Creation**

Becoming a seller on Amazon.in requires an initial setup of a seller account through either **sell.amazon.in** or **sellercentral.amazon.in**.

### **Account Initiation**

* **Existing Customers:** Individuals with an existing Amazon.in customer account or Prime Video account can utilize the same email and password to initiate a seller account.
* **New Users:** Users without an account must select "Create your Amazon account" and provide their name, mobile number, email address, and a unique password.

### **Essential Registration Checklist**

Before beginning the registration workflow, applicants must have the following information and documents ready:

* **Email ID:** A valid electronic mail address for account communications.
* **Active Mobile Number:** Required for verification and security.
* **GST Number:** Mandatory for most sellers unless exclusively operating in GST-exempt categories.
* **PAN Details:** Required for tax purposes; mandatory for sellers of GST-exempt goods who do not have a GSTIN.
* **Active Bank Account:** Used for the disbursement of funds from sales.

### **### Common Account Registration Mistakes**

- Registering with an incorrect PAN or GSTIN.
- Providing a bank account that does not belong to the registered business.
- Using an inactive email address or phone number.
- Uploading blurry or expired documents.
- Selecting the wrong business type during registration.

### **### Account Registration Best Practices**

- Keep all documents ready before beginning registration.
- Ensure names match exactly across PAN, GST, and bank account.
- Use an email address that will remain active for business communication.
- Enable Two-Step Verification immediately after account creation.

## **Security Protocol: Two-Step Verification (2SV)**

Two-Step Verification is a mandatory security layer required to access Seller Central and protect against unauthorized account access.

### **Operational Workflow**

1. **Standard Login:** Users enter their standard email and password.
2. **Secondary Code:** A unique code is sent to a designated device via SMS, voice call, or an authenticator app.
3. **Authenticator Apps:** These are recommended as they are free, easy to use, and do not require network access.

### **Device and Method Requirements**

Users must designate a **Primary Method** and a **Backup Method**. These cannot utilize the same telephone number.

* **If Primary is SMS:** Backup must be a different voice phone, a different SMS phone, or an authenticator app.
* **If Primary is Voice:** Backup must be a different voice phone, a different SMS phone, or an authenticator app.
* **If Primary is Authenticator App:** Backup must be an SMS-enabled or voice-enabled phone.

## **Tax Registration: Goods and Services Tax (GST)**

GST is an indirect tax that subsumes service tax, VAT, excise duty, customs duty, and entry tax into a single system.

### **GST Mandates for E-commerce**

* **Threshold Irrelevance:** GST registration under the "normal category" is compulsory for selling goods on e-commerce platforms, regardless of whether the business meets the standard aggregate turnover threshold (INR 40 lakhs or 20 lakhs).
* **GSTIN:** Once registered, entities receive a unique 15-digit **GST Identification Number (GSTIN)** used to monitor tax payments and compliance.
* **Benefits:** Registration allows for smooth tax collection and the ability to avail of Input Tax Credit on inbound supplies.

### **Why GST Matters**

GST registration enables sellers to legally sell products on Amazon India, collect applicable taxes, and claim Input Tax Credit where eligible. Incorrect GST information may delay verification, prevent listings from becoming active, or result in compliance issues.

### **GST-Exempt Categories**

Sellers exclusively offering specific goods may not be required to provide a GSTIN and can instead provide PAN details. Exemptions are either **Absolute** (unconditional) or **Conditional** (based on specific terms in the GST Act).

**Common Exempt Items:**

* **Food:** Unpacked cereals, fresh fruits/vegetables, salt, non-branded flour, milk, curd, and unprocessed tea/coffee.
* **Raw Materials:** Raw silk, jute, wool, and firewood.
* **Educational/Misc:** Books, maps, journals, child coloring books, bangles, and the Indian national flag.
* **Tools:** Hearing aids and agricultural hand tools.

## **GST Registration Document Requirements**

Documents required vary based on the business constitution (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Company).

### **Common Documents for All Business Types**

* **PAN Card:** Permanent Account Number of the applicant/authorized signatories. Companies or HUFs must provide a specific entity PAN.
* **Aadhaar Card:** Important for individuals and sole proprietors.
* **Photographs:** Passport-sized photos of all owners/signatories (JPEG format, max 100KB for online uploads).
* **Contact Information:** Valid phone number and email for the Primary Authorized Signatory.
* **Proof of Principal Place of Business:** Recent property tax receipt, electricity bill, municipal khata, ownership deed, or a valid rent/lease agreement.
* **Bank Account Details:** Not mandatory at the initial application; can be added later via an amendment application. Requires a bank statement or passbook showing the IFSC and branch address.

### **Specific Documents by Business Class**

* **Partnerships:** Deed of Partnership.
* **Traders/Service Providers:** A concise list of the taxable goods and services being provided.
* **Authorized Signatories:** Letter of authorization or a copy of a board resolution.
* **LLPs and Companies:** Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) of the authorized signee.
* **Incorporation:** Incorporation Certificate from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs or Memorandum of Association (MOA).
* **Non-Residents:** Scanned copy of Passport and Visa details; registration number from the home country if the entity is incorporated outside India.
* **Online Service Providers (No India Location):** Clearance certificate from the Govt. of India or a trade license from the home country.

## **GST Online Registration Workflow**

The registration process is entirely digital and paperless.

### **Part I: Generating the Application (TRN)**

1. Visit the official GST portal and navigate to **Services \> Registration \> New Registration**.
2. Enter details (PAN, email, mobile) to receive OTPs for verification.
3. Upon verification, a **Temporary Reference Number (TRN)** is generated.

### **Part II: Form Submission**

1. Log in using the TRN to access the application form.
2. Complete the 10 sections/tabs within **15 days**; otherwise, the TRN and saved data will be deleted.
3. Provide proof of constitution and authorized signatory details.

### **Part III: Verification and Digital Signatures**

* **DSC:** Mandatory for Companies and LLPs. Requires DSC software, a DSC Dongle, and Emsigner.
* **Alternative Methods:** Verification can also be completed via e-signature or EVC.
* **ARN:** Completion generates an **Application Reference Number (ARN)** for tracking.

### **Common GST Registration Issues**

- OTP not received during verification.
- PAN details do not match government records.
- GST verification remains pending.
- Incorrect business constitution selected.
- DSC installation problems for companies and LLPs.

### **Troubleshooting**

- Verify PAN and GST information before submission.
- Re-upload clear document scans.
- Wait up to 72 hours for GST verification.
- Contact Seller Support if verification remains pending beyond the expected timeframe.

## **Seller Central Store Configuration**

### **Store Name Policies**

* **Uniqueness:** The store name must be unique. If an error states "Store name already exists," a different name must be chosen.
* **Visibility:** The name represents the business to customers in search results and on the storefront.
* **Flexibility:** The store name can be updated at any time through account settings after registration is complete.
* **Limitation:** Only one store name is permitted per seller account. Multiple names require separate accounts, each with its own unique GSTIN.

### **Best Practices for Choosing Store Name**

- Choose a professional and memorable store name.
- Avoid names that violate trademarks.
- Keep the name simple and easy to search.
- Ensure the name accurately represents your business.

### **Pickup Address Compliance**

* **State Alignment:** The pickup address **must be in the same state** as the registered GST.
* **Operational Role:** This is the location where carriers will collect product shipments for customer orders.
* **Modification:** The address can be changed post-registration as long as it remains within the same GST-registered state.

### **Bank Account and Disbursements**

* **Verification:** Sellers must provide the account holder's name (matching the PAN), account number, and IFSC code.
* **Purpose:** This account is used for the electronic transfer of payments due to the seller.

### **Common Bank Mistakes**

- Bank account holder name differs from PAN.
- Incorrect IFSC code.
- Closed or inactive bank account.
- Business account not matching the registered entity.

## **Tax Compliance: Product Tax Codes (PTC)**

The **Product Tax Code** is the government-notified tax rate applicable to a specific category of goods or services.

* **Default Settings:** Sellers must select a default PTC during registration. This code will automatically calculate taxes on listings where a specific tax code is not manually selected.
* **Multiple Categories:** If selling across multiple categories with different rates, sellers should select the PTC applicable to the majority of their products. Individual rates can be adjusted for other categories during product listing.

## **Final Compliance and Launch Requirements**

A seller's registration is not considered fully "launched" until specific operational steps are satisfied.

### **Verification Timeline**

* **GST Verification:** This process can take up to **72 hours**. Sellers can continue with other registration steps while the review is pending.
* **Verification Failure:** If automatic verification fails, sellers must upload the official GSTIN certificate (Reg-06 with Annexures A and B) in PDF, JPG, or DOC format (under 10MB).

### **Mandatory Pre-Launch Step**

To launch the store on Amazon.in, a seller must complete **at least one product listing**. This listing provides the necessary photos, descriptions, and pricing for customer evaluation. Once this is completed, the "Start Selling" button becomes active, making products available to customers.

### **Fulfillment for FBA Sellers**

Sellers joining Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) must perform additional registration-related compliance by adding the chosen Amazon Fulfillment Centers (FC) as an **Additional Place of Business (APOB)** in their GST registration.

### **Seller Readiness Checklist**

Before selling, confirm that:

- Seller account is verified.
- GST verification is complete.
- Bank account is verified.
- Pickup address is configured.
- Product Tax Code is selected.
- At least one product listing has been created.
- Two-Step Verification is enabled.
- Required tax documents are available.

> **Important**
>
> Amazon may request additional verification documents depending on the business type, product category, or compliance requirements. Sellers should periodically review Seller Central notifications to ensure their account remains compliant.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Can I register without GST?**

Only sellers exclusively dealing in GST-exempt products may register without a GSTIN by providing PAN details, subject to Amazon's eligibility requirements.

### **Can I change my store name later?**

Yes. The store name can be updated through Seller Central after registration, provided it complies with Amazon policies.

### **How long does GST verification take?**

GST verification typically takes up to 72 hours. If automatic verification fails, supporting documents may be requested.

### **Is Two-Step Verification mandatory?**

Yes. Two-Step Verification is required to access Seller Central and helps protect seller accounts from unauthorized access.
