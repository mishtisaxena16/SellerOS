# Flipkart Seller Getting Started Guide

## Overview

Flipkart enables sellers to list and sell products across thousands of categories and reach customers across India.

The seller journey typically involves:

1. Creating a seller account
2. Listing products
3. Managing fulfillment and shipping
4. Receiving payments
5. Scaling the business using Flipkart growth tools

---

# Seller Account Registration

## Required Information

To register as a seller, you generally need:

- Business information
- Contact details (email and phone number)
- GSTIN for applicable categories
- PAN for book sellers
- Bank account details
- Pickup address

---

## GST Registration

If you do not have a GSTIN:

1. Register on the GST Portal.
2. Complete the GST application process.
3. Submit required information.
4. Obtain GST registration before selling in applicable categories.

# Product Listings

## What is a Listing?

A listing is the process of registering a product on Flipkart so that customers can view and purchase it.

A complete listing typically contains:

- Product title
- Product description
- Product images
- Pricing information
- Product specifications
- Features and attributes

Accurate information and high-quality images improve visibility and customer trust.

---

## Listing Methods

### Match Existing Product

If the product already exists on Flipkart:

- Link your offer to the existing listing
- Avoid duplicate catalogs
- Start selling faster

### Create New Product Listing

If the product does not exist:

Provide:

- Product description
- Specifications
- Features
- Dimensions
- Product images
- Usage instructions

A new catalog will be created for the product.

---

## Brand Approval

Certain products and brands may require approval before listing.

Typical requirements include:

- Brand ownership proof
- Authorization documents
- Product category details

# Fulfillment and Shipping

Flipkart provides two fulfillment models.

## Fulfillment by Flipkart (FBF)

FBF allows Flipkart to manage storage, packing, shipping, and delivery.

### Benefits

- Pickup from seller location
- Warehouse storage support
- Faster delivery
- FAssured eligibility
- Seamless order processing
- End-to-end return handling
- Quality checks
- Packaging support
- Product selection recommendations

### Suitable For

Sellers who want Flipkart to handle logistics operations.

---

## Non-Fulfillment by Flipkart (NFBF)

Under NFBF, sellers manage packaging and dispatch preparation while Flipkart handles delivery.

### Benefits

- Nationwide delivery
- Tracking support
- Returns support
- Logistics assistance
- Greater inventory control

### Seller Responsibilities

- Proper packaging
- Order readiness before pickup
- Timely dispatch preparation

# Payments

## Seller Payments

Payments are credited directly to the seller's registered bank account.

### Key Points

- Payments are processed after successful order fulfillment
- Settlement timelines depend on platform policy
- Sellers can track payments through Seller Hub
