# Flipkart Shipping & Fulfillment Guide

## Overview

Shipping and fulfillment directly impact customer satisfaction, delivery performance, and seller ratings.

Delivery timelines depend on:

- Product availability
- Seller location
- Customer location
- Logistics partner serviceability
- Public holidays and non-working days

Business days generally exclude Sundays and public holidays.

---

# Delivery Timelines

## Factors Affecting Delivery

Estimated delivery dates may vary based on:

- Product availability
- Seller processing time
- Distance between seller and customer
- Logistics network performance
- Local serviceability constraints
- Public holidays

Delivery timelines shown on product listings are estimates and may change due to operational conditions.

---

## Why Delivery Dates Vary

Different sellers may show different delivery estimates because of:

- Inventory availability
- Warehouse location
- Seller location
- Courier partner capabilities
- Customer delivery location

Faster fulfillment generally improves customer satisfaction and conversion rates.

---

# Shipping Serviceability

## Delivery Coverage

Delivery availability depends on:

- Seller shipping coverage
- Logistics partner availability
- Customer pin code
- Product category restrictions
- Regional regulations

Not all products can be delivered to every location.

---

## Non-Serviceable Locations

A location may become non-serviceable due to:

- Logistics limitations
- Seller shipping restrictions
- Regulatory restrictions
- Operational constraints

Sellers should monitor serviceability and shipping performance regularly.

---

# Cash on Delivery (COD)

## COD Availability

Cash on Delivery availability depends on:

- Delivery partner support
- Customer location
- Order value limits
- Operational restrictions

Not all pin codes support COD services.

---

# Order Fulfillment Responsibilities

## Seller Responsibilities

Sellers should:

- Maintain accurate inventory
- Process orders promptly
- Prepare products for shipment
- Use proper packaging
- Ensure correct shipping information

Timely processing helps reduce cancellations and improve customer experience.

---

# Return Pickups

## Return Process

When customers request returns:

1. Return request is initiated.
2. Pickup may be arranged through Flipkart logistics.
3. If pickup is unavailable, alternate courier arrangements may be used.
4. Return handling follows Flipkart return policies.

Return logistics may vary by product category and location.

---

# Delivery Exceptions

## Delivered Status but Customer Did Not Receive Product

In certain situations, customers may report non-delivery despite delivery confirmation.

Such cases are investigated through seller support and logistics teams.

Maintaining shipment records and tracking information helps resolve disputes efficiently.

---

# Inventory Availability Status

Understanding catalog availability statuses helps sellers manage inventory effectively.

## In Stock

The product is available and ready for shipment.

Characteristics:

- Immediate order acceptance
- Standard delivery timelines
- Higher conversion potential

---

## Available

The product is not immediately available but can be procured after order placement.

Characteristics:

- Additional procurement time required
- Longer delivery timelines

---

## Preorder

The product is available for advance booking before official release.

Characteristics:

- Ships after launch date
- Delivery begins after product release

---

## Out of Stock

The product is temporarily unavailable.

Recommended action:

- Replenish inventory quickly
- Monitor stock levels regularly

---

## Back In Stock Soon

Inventory replenishment is expected shortly.

Customers may choose to wait for availability.

---

## Temporarily Unavailable

Product is unavailable for purchase at present but may return in the future.

---

## Permanently Discontinued

The product is no longer available for sale.

Reasons may include:

- Product discontinuation
- Obsolete inventory
- Manufacturer withdrawal

---

# Imported Products

Imported products may require additional procurement and shipping time.

Considerations:

- Longer delivery timelines
- Customs and sourcing delays
- Inventory planning requirements

---

# International Shipping

Currently, Flipkart primarily supports deliveries within India.

Sellers should focus on:

- Domestic fulfillment
- Serviceable pin codes
- Efficient nationwide delivery coverage

---

# Shipping Best Practices

- Maintain accurate inventory levels.
- Process orders quickly.
- Use secure packaging.
- Monitor shipment tracking regularly.
- Minimize dispatch delays.
- Reduce order cancellations.
- Keep product availability updated.
- Monitor delivery performance metrics.
- Ensure compliance with Flipkart fulfillment requirements.

Successful fulfillment depends on:

- Fast order processing
- Accurate inventory management
- Reliable packaging
- Timely dispatch
- Consistent delivery performance
