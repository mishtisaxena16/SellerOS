# Flipkart Product Image & Listing Guidelines

## Overview

Product images are one of the most important factors influencing customer purchase decisions.

Since customers cannot physically inspect products before purchasing, high-quality images help:

- Increase customer trust
- Improve product visibility
- Improve conversion rates
- Reduce customer confusion
- Lower return rates
- Enhance overall catalog quality

---

# Types of Product Photography

## 1. Individual Product Images

Show a single product clearly against a clean background.

Best practices:

- Use high-resolution images
- Capture multiple angles
- Keep focus on the product
- Avoid distractions in the frame

Purpose:

- Clearly showcase the product
- Help customers understand appearance and design

---

## 2. Product Usage Images

Demonstrate how the product is used in real-life situations.

Examples:

- Clothing worn by a model
- Sunglasses being worn
- Home products shown in use
- Kitchen products used in real settings

Benefits:

- Helps customers visualize usage
- Creates stronger purchase intent
- Improves product understanding

---

## 3. Multi-Product Images

Show multiple related products together.

Benefits:

- Highlights product variety
- Encourages cross-selling
- Improves brand presentation

Use only when products are related and clearly visible.

---

## 4. Size Reference Images

Help customers understand product dimensions.

Examples:

- Product beside common household objects
- Product held by a model
- Scale comparison images

Benefits:

- Reduces size-related confusion
- Improves customer expectations
- Reduces returns caused by misunderstanding

---

## 5. Packaging Images

Show how the product is packaged.

Images may include:

- Product box
- Packaging material
- Labels
- Brand information
- Product information stickers

Benefits:

- Builds customer confidence
- Demonstrates authenticity
- Provides additional product information

---

## 6. Feature Highlight Images

Use close-up images to showcase important product details.

Examples:

- Material quality
- Fabric texture
- Buttons and zippers
- Product finish
- Design elements
- Technical features

Benefits:

- Highlights product quality
- Improves customer understanding
- Increases perceived value

---

# Product Photography Best Practices

## Maintain High Image Quality

Images should be:

- High resolution
- Sharp and clear
- Well focused
- Free from blur
- Free from pixelation

Poor-quality images can negatively impact customer trust and sales.

---

## Use the Right Background

Recommended background characteristics:

- Clean
- Simple
- Non-distracting
- Contrasting with the product

White backgrounds are generally preferred because they:

- Improve product visibility
- Create a professional appearance
- Maintain consistency across catalogs

---

## Ensure Proper Lighting

Good lighting is essential for accurate product representation.

Guidelines:

- Avoid overly dark images
- Avoid excessive brightness
- Ensure true product colors are visible
- Minimize harsh shadows
- Maintain image clarity

Both natural and artificial lighting can be effective when properly controlled.

---

## Capture Multiple Angles

Products should be photographed from different perspectives.

Recommended views:

- Front view
- Side view
- Back view
- Top view
- Detail view
- Close-up view

Benefits:

- Improves product understanding
- Reduces customer uncertainty
- Increases purchase confidence

---

## Use Realistic Product Representation

Product images should accurately represent the actual product.

Guidelines:

- Avoid misleading edits
- Show actual colors
- Demonstrate realistic usage
- Maintain authenticity

Accurate images help reduce customer complaints and returns.

---

# Image Optimization

Before uploading images:

- Use appropriate image dimensions
- Maintain image quality after compression
- Ensure platform-compatible file sizes
- Avoid unnecessary image editing
- Preserve visual clarity

Optimized images improve loading performance while maintaining quality.

---

# Catalog Quality Recommendations

A high-quality catalog should include:

- Clear product title
- Accurate product description
- Multiple product images
- Usage images where relevant
- Feature-focused images
- Size reference images when applicable

Together, these elements improve discoverability and customer trust.

---

# Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid:

- Blurry images
- Low-resolution images
- Poor lighting
- Distracting backgrounds
- Misleading product representation
- Missing product angles
- Excessive editing
- Inaccurate colors
- Cropped or incomplete product views

These issues may reduce product performance and customer confidence.

---

# Key Seller Takeaways

High-quality product photography can:

- Increase visibility
- Improve customer engagement
- Improve conversion rates
- Reduce returns
- Strengthen brand credibility
- Enhance overall catalog performance

Product images should clearly and accurately represent the product while helping customers make informed purchasing decisions.
