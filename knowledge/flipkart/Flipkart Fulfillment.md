How Does Fulfilment by Flipkart Work?

There is an integrated approach that ensures a seamless operation from order placement to delivery,
which enhances both seller and customer satisfaction. This includes:

Order Fulfilment

When a customer places an order, FBF immediately takes action. The process begins with the precise
picking of the product from the inventory. It is then securely packed to ensure it reaches the
customer in perfect condition. Following this, the order is dispatched swiftly from the nearest
fulfilment centre to ensure rapid delivery, fulfilling customer expectations with efficiency.

Inventory Management

FBF stores sellers’ products in strategically located warehouses across the country. This thoughtful
placement not only safeguards the inventory but also optimises logistics by positioning products
closer to potential buyers. The result is reduced delivery times and lowered costs, making logistics
smoother and more cost-effective for sellers.

Product Tracking

Throughout this process, sellers have the ability to monitor their products in real time. FBF provides
continuous updates on inventory status, order processing, and shipments. This real-time tracking

Register as a Seller

Begin by signing up on the Flipkart Seller platform if you are not already a registered seller. Visit the
Flipkart Seller homepage, click on 'Register' or 'Sign Up', and provide the necessary business details,
such as your name, email, phone number, and GSTIN, to create your account.

Express Interest in FBF

Once you have an active seller account, log in to the Flipkart Seller portal. Navigate to 'Manage

Approval and Registration

After expressing interest, Flipkart will review your account performance metrics. If approved, you will
receive an invitation to register for FBF. Upon acceptance, specify Flipkart's Fulfilment Centre (FC) as
your principal or additional business space.

Consignment Creation

Within your seller dashboard, create a consignment listing the products you wish to send to the FC.
This includes detailing product types, quantities, and other relevant information.

Ship Your Products

After creating your consignment, ship your products to the designated fulfilment centre. Ensure that
your products are packed securely and labelled correctly to facilitate easy processing at the FC.

Quality Checks

Upon receiving your products, Flipkart conducts thorough quality checks to ensure all items meet
their standards. Products that pass these checks are then moved inward and ready for storage in the
FC.

Storage and Order Fulfilment

Once stored, your products are managed entirely by Flipkart. As orders are placed, Flipkart will
handle the picking, packing, and shipping of products directly to customers.

Payment Settlement

The payments for orders processed through FBF are settled according to Flipkart's regular payment

FAQs on Fulfilment by Flipkart

Is FBF applicable across all product categories?

Fulfilment by Flipkart covers a wide range of products; however, not all categories are eligible for this
service. The eligibility of products for FBF depends on specific criteria set by Flipkart, which can
include the type of product and other factors.

How does Flipkart assure product quality in FBF warehouses?

Quality assurance in FBF warehouses is rigorous. Flipkart staff conduct regular quality checks on
products stored in their fulfilment centres to ensure they meet high standards before they are
shipped to customers. This process helps maintain product integrity and customer satisfaction.

What is the difference between FBF and NFBF?

There are two Flipkart fulfilment types. This includes:

Fulfilment by Flipkart (FBF): This is an all-inclusive service in which Flipkart manages the storage,
packaging, and shipping of products, providing a seamless end-to-end solution for sellers. Due to
Flipkart's extensive logistics network, this approach often results in quicker delivery times.

Non-fulfilment by Flipkart (NFBF): This allows sellers more control as they handle their own
packaging, store products at their own or community warehouses, and coordinate with Flipkart for
pickup and delivery. It is basically merchant fulfilment.
