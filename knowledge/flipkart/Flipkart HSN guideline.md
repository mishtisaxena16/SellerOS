What is the HSN Code?

HSN Codes, or Harmonized System of Nomenclature Codes, are a standardised system of names and
numbers to classify traded products. Developed by the World WCO, they are used by more than 200
countries worldwide, making them a global language for trade. Intriguingly, this system categorises
over 5,000 commodity groups, each identified by a six-digit code. They are organised in a legal and
logical manner, following a set of well-established rules.

How do HSN Codes Work?

Also known as HSN numbers, these codes function as a universal language for products, ensuring a
seamless process of classification and taxation. Each code is a unique identifier, meticulously crafted
to represent a specific product. It typically consists of six digits, though it can be extended to eight
for further specificity.

Businesses use these codes to determine the applicable tax rate for their products. When a business
files for input tax credit, the HSN code helps tax authorities easily understand the nature of the
goods. This systematic approach not only simplifies tax calculation but also aids in trade.

How Does the HSN Code Work Under GST?

HSN code in GST brings clarity and efficiency to taxing goods, making it easier for businesses and
authorities alike. They are a key instrument in gathering data and analysing global trade patterns.
Furthermore, they ensure compliance and smooth operation for Indian businesses.

Different Types of HSN Numbers/Codes in India

The HSN code list under GST has a unique categorisation tailored to the country's diverse business
landscape. Here is the three-tiered structure followed across all states.

2-digit HSN code:

Applicable to businesses with a turnover between ₹1.5 and ₹5 crores, this simplified HSN code
system categorises goods, providing a broader view of product classification for easier GST
compliance. Specifically designed for medium-sized enterprises in India, it streamlines product
categorization and reduces complexities.

4-digit HSN code:

Expanding on the 2-digit code, this one offers a more specific classification. It includes the heading
number, providing a clearer indication of the product group under a given chapter. This level of detail
is generally used by larger businesses in India with turnover exceeding ₹5 crores.

6-digit HSN code:

This is for larger businesses with turnover exceeding ₹5 crores in the previous financial year. It is
globally standardised, ensuring consistency in the classification of goods for international trade. It is
extensively used in India for a detailed and accurate representation of goods in the GST regime.

8-digit HSN code:

Aligning with the Indian Tariff Code (ITC), this detailed level is used for the precise identification of
goods. This level's crucial role in customs and import-export ensures accurate duty calculations,
efficient trade controls, and seamless regulatory compliance.

Small businesses with an annual turnover of less than ₹1.5 crores are exempt from using HSN codes
in India.

FAQs on HSN Code:

Who is eligible to use HSN codes?

Any business that is registered under GST in India is eligible to use HSN codes. The requirement to
use them varies based on annual turnover, with different thresholds necessitating different levels.

Is it mandatory to use HSN codes?

Yes, it is mandatory for businesses registered under GST to use HSN codes. The requirement for the
level (2-digit, 4-digit, 6-digit, or 8-digit) depends on the annual turnover of the business.

What is the purpose of using HSN codes?

HSN codes are used to standardise the classification of goods, simplify tax filing, ensure accurate GST
rates are applied, and facilitate international trade. They help in streamlining the taxation process
and in policy-making by providing clear data on goods classification.

How many digits are there in HSN codes?

HSN codes can be 2, 4, 6, or 8 digits long. The number of digits represents the level of specificity and
detail in the product classification, with 8-digit codes being the most specific.

How to register the HSN code and GST rate?

Registering HSN codes and GST rates is part of the GST registration process. Businesses should
classify their goods according to the code and apply the corresponding GST rate when filing their tax
returns. Also, make sure you have all the right documents required for GST registration.

Are HSN codes different for each country?

The first 6 digits of HSN codes are standardised globally. However, countries can extend these codes
to 8 or 10 digits for more specific classifications, leading to variations in the detailed categorisation of
goods.

Do B2B and B2C businesses have different HSN codes?

B2B and B2C businesses use the same HSN codes for product classification. The difference lies in the
level of detail required in invoices, with B2B transactions generally requiring more detailed codes.

What happens if the incorrect HSN code is used?

Using an incorrect HSN code can lead to wrong GST calculations, resulting in tax discrepancies. It may
attract penalties and can complicate compliance and audit processes.

What is the importance of HSN codes during GST returns?

HSN codes are crucial in GST returns for accurate tax rate application, compliance, and facilitating
audits. They ensure the proper categorisation of goods, leading to precise tax calculations and
streamlined return filing

Is it possible for HSN codes to change over time?

Yes, they can change over time. The WCO reviews the HSN system every five years, potentially
leading to updates or changes in product classifications and codes.
