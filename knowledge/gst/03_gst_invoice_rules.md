# GST Invoice Rules

## Overview

GST invoice rules define how businesses should issue invoices, bills of supply, receipts, debit notes, and delivery challans while complying with GST regulations.

---

# 1. Tax Invoice

A GST tax invoice must contain:

- Supplier name, address and GSTIN
- Invoice number (unique for the financial year)
- Invoice date
- Recipient name, address and GSTIN/UIN (if registered)
- Delivery address (if different)
- HSN Code (Goods) or SAC Code (Services)
- Description of goods/services
- Quantity (for goods)
- Total invoice value
- Taxable value after discounts
- GST rate (CGST/SGST/IGST/Cess)
- GST amount
- Place of supply (for interstate transactions)
- Reverse charge indication (if applicable)
- Signature or Digital Signature

---

## Export Invoices

Export invoices should include one of the following endorsements:

- Supply Meant for Export on Payment of IGST
- Supply Meant for Export under Bond/LUT without Payment of IGST

Additional details required:

- Recipient name
- Delivery address
- Destination country
- Export application reference

---

## Small Value Supplies

If the invoice value is below ₹200:

- Invoice is not mandatory if the customer is unregistered.
- Customer does not request an invoice.
- A consolidated invoice may be issued at the end of the day.

---

# 2. Time Limit for Issuing Tax Invoice

### Goods

Invoice should generally be issued before or at the time of removal of goods.

### Services

Invoice should be issued within **30 days** from the date of supply.

For banks, insurers and NBFCs:

- Invoice may be issued within **45 days**.

---

# 3. Invoice Copies

### Goods

Prepare three copies:

- Original – Recipient
- Duplicate – Transporter
- Triplicate – Supplier

### Services

Prepare two copies:

- Original – Recipient
- Duplicate – Supplier

Invoice details should also be reported in **GSTR-1**.

---

# 4. Bill of Supply

Used when:

- Supplying exempt goods/services
- Composition Scheme taxpayers

A Bill of Supply should include:

- Supplier details
- Invoice number
- Date
- Recipient details
- Description
- HSN/SAC (where applicable)
- Value
- Signature

No Bill of Supply is required for supplies below ₹200.

---

# 5. Receipt Voucher

Issued when advance payment is received.

Must include:

- Supplier details
- Voucher number
- Date
- Recipient details
- Advance amount
- GST rate
- GST amount
- Place of supply
- Reverse charge (if applicable)

---

# 6. Debit Notes, Credit Notes & Revised Invoices

These documents should include:

- Document type
- Supplier details
- Recipient details
- Original invoice reference
- Revised taxable value
- GST amount
- Signature

### Revised Invoice

Can be issued:

- From effective registration date
- Until GST registration certificate is issued

### Important

Invoices issued under GST penalty proceedings should clearly mention:

**"INPUT TAX CREDIT NOT ADMISSIBLE"**

---

# 7. Special Cases

## Banking & Financial Institutions

Invoices:

- Need not be serially numbered
- Need not contain recipient address
- Must contain other prescribed information

---

## Goods Transport Agency (GTA)

Invoice should additionally contain:

- Gross weight
- Consignor details
- Consignee details
- Vehicle number
- Place of origin
- Destination
- GSTIN of person liable to pay tax

---

## Passenger Transport

Tickets issued by airlines, railways or bus operators are treated as tax invoices.

---

# 8. Transportation without Tax Invoice

A Delivery Challan may be issued instead of a Tax Invoice for:

- Job Work
- Transportation without sale
- Liquid Gas
- Other notified cases

A Delivery Challan should include:

- Challan Number
- Date
- Consignor Details
- Consignee Details
- Description of Goods
- Quantity
- Taxable Value
- GST Details
- Place of Supply
- Signature

---

## Delivery Challan Copies

Prepare three copies:

- Original – Consignee
- Duplicate – Transporter
- Triplicate – Consigner

---

## Semi Knocked Down (SKD) / Completely Knocked Down (CKD)

Requirements:

- Complete invoice before dispatch of first consignment
- Delivery Challan for remaining consignments
- Certified invoice copy with every consignment
- Original invoice with final consignment

---

# Best Practices

- Use unique invoice numbers.
- Verify GSTIN before issuing invoices.
- Mention HSN/SAC codes correctly.
- Issue invoices within prescribed timelines.
- Keep invoice records safely.
- File invoice details accurately in GSTR-1.

---

# Common Seller Mistakes

- Missing GSTIN
- Incorrect HSN/SAC code
- Wrong GST calculation
- Missing place of supply
- Duplicate invoice numbers
- Late invoice generation
- Incorrect invoice format
