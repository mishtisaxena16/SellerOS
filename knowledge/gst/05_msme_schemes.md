# MSME Design Support Scheme (CLCS-TUS)

## Overview

The Design Support Scheme under the Credit Linked Capital Subsidy and Technology Upgradation Scheme (CLCS-TUS) helps manufacturing MSMEs improve product design, innovation, quality, branding, and competitiveness.
The objective is to enable MSMEs to move from contract manufacturing to original product and brand development.

---

## Objectives

The scheme aims to:
- Promote design-driven innovation
- Improve product quality
- Develop new products
- Increase manufacturing competitiveness
- Encourage sustainable product design
- Strengthen branding and market value

---

## Eligibility

Eligible applicants include:
- Manufacturing MSMEs
- Enterprises registered under MSME regulations
Applicants should preferably have an operational business with a positive performance record.

---

## Design Awareness Programs

Financial assistance is available for:

### Awareness Programmes

Support:
- Up to ₹70,000 per programme

### Design Workshops

Support:
- Up to ₹5 lakh

Government Funding:
- 75%

Workshop Duration:
- Up to 5 days

---

### National Workshops

Support:
- Up to ₹5 lakh

---

### Orientation Programmes

Support:
- Up to ₹2 lakh

---

## Design Projects

MSMEs can receive financial support for hiring professional design consultants.

Project Cost:
- ₹15 lakh to ₹40 lakh

Government Assistance:
- 75% for Micro Enterprises
- 60% for Small & Medium Enterprises

Funding is released in three stages:

1. Strategy & Concept (40%)
2. Detailed Design (30%)
3. Final Implementation (30%)

---

## Student Design Projects

Final-year design students may undertake projects for MSMEs.

Government Support:
- Up to 75% of eligible project cost
- Maximum ₹1.5 lakh

MSME Contribution:
- 25%

Supported expenses include:
- Prototype development
- Documentation
- Model making
- Student stipend (where applicable)

---

## Project Duration

Projects should normally be completed within one year.

---

## Benefits for MSMEs

The scheme helps businesses:
- Improve product design
- Increase product quality
- Build stronger brands
- Develop innovative products
- Enhance customer value
- Improve manufacturing efficiency
- Promote sustainable product development

---

## Key Takeaways
The Design Support Scheme enables manufacturing MSMEs to access expert design consultancy with government financial assistance, helping them develop innovative, competitive, and market-ready products.
