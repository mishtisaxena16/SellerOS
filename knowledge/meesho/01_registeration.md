# Meesho Seller Registration

## Overview

Meesho allows businesses and individuals to sell products through its supplier platform.

Sellers can register using:

- Regular GSTIN
- Composition GSTIN
- Enrolment ID / UIN (for eligible sellers without GST)

---

## Eligibility

You can register on Meesho if you have:

- A Regular GSTIN
- A Composition GSTIN
- An Enrolment ID / UIN (where applicable)

Sellers without GST registration can sell within their own state after obtaining the required Enrolment ID/UIN.

---

## Documents Required

Before registration, keep the following ready:

- GSTIN or Enrolment ID/UIN
- Active bank account
- Mobile number
- Email address

---

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Create Account

1. Visit the Meesho Supplier registration portal.
2. Enter your business mobile number.
3. Verify using OTP.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Create a password.

Password requirements:

- Minimum 8 characters
- At least 1 uppercase letter
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 special character

### Step 2: Verify GSTIN / Enrolment ID

1. Enter GSTIN or applicable registration identifier.
2. Verify details.
3. Continue.

### Step 3: Pickup Address

1. Enter pickup address.
2. Confirm details.
3. Continue.

### Step 4: Bank Verification

Provide:

- Account number
- IFSC code

### Step 5: Business Details

Provide:

- Store name
- Seller name

Accept terms and submit.

---

## Supplier Panel Setup

After registration:

1. Access Supplier Panel.
2. Upload product catalogs.
3. Configure listings.
4. Start selling.

---

## Catalog Upload Methods

### Single Catalog Upload

1. Select Catalog Upload.
2. Add Single Catalog.
3. Choose category.
4. Upload images.
5. Enter specifications.
6. Submit for review.

### Bulk Catalog Upload

1. Select Bulk Upload.
2. Choose category.
3. Download template.
4. Fill required fields.
5. Upload completed file.
6. Submit for review.

Important:

- Avoid duplicate products.
- Use required dropdown values.
- Follow template instructions.

---

## Seller Success Tips

### Catalog Coverage

Maintain multiple catalogs to improve visibility.

### Competitive Pricing

Consider:

- Product cost
- Profit margin
- Market competition

### Market Trends

Regularly add products that match current demand.

### Next-Day Dispatch (NDD)

Products dispatched the next day may receive better visibility.

---

## Payments and Settlement

Settlement Amount = Product Price - Commission - GST - Applicable Charges

Payments are transferred to the registered bank account according to platform settlement policies.
