# Meesho Catalog Upload Guide

## Overview

Meesho allows sellers to upload product catalogs using either:

1. Description and Image Upload
2. Excel Template Upload

Catalog approval depends on providing accurate product information, correct category selection, proper pricing, and valid product images.

---

## Catalog Approval Guidelines

Follow these guidelines for faster approvals:

### Product Description

Include all information a customer needs before purchasing:

- Material
- Color
- Pattern
- Size or dimensions
- Product type
- Price
- Other relevant specifications

Incorrect descriptions may lead to increased returns.

### Category Selection

- Upload products only under the correct category.
- Products should match the selected category.

### Product Count

- Include at least 6 products in a catalog where possible.
- More products may improve upload efficiency and catalog visibility.

### Pricing

Final selling price should include:

- GST
- Platform commission

Do not include shipping charges, as shipping costs are not charged to sellers separately.

### Images

- Upload clear product images.
- Images can be uploaded directly or through the Excel template.
- Ensure images accurately represent the product.

### File Upload

- Follow the provided template format.
- Enter all required information before submission.

---

# Method 1: Upload Using Description and Images

## Step 1: Add Product Description

Product description should include:

- Material
- Color
- Pattern
- Size or dimensions
- Product type
- Price
- Relevant product details

If products have different prices, mention them clearly in the description.

---

## Step 2: Select Category

Choose the most appropriate category for the product.

---

## Step 3: Enter Starting Price

Enter the lowest price available within the catalog.

Price should already include:

- GST
- Platform commission

---

## Step 4: Upload Images

1. Click Add Images.
2. Open the upload window.
3. Select product images.
4. Submit the catalog.

Catalog size requirements:

- Minimum: 4 products
- Maximum: 18 products

---

# Method 2: Upload Using Excel Template

## Step 1: Fill Product Details

Complete all required fields in the template.

Required information may include:

- SKU ID
- Fabric or material
- Size chart
- Pricing
- Product specifications

---

## Step 2: Select Category

Choose the correct category before proceeding.

---

## Step 3: Enter Starting Price

Enter the lowest catalog price.

Price should include:

- GST
- Platform commission

Do not include shipping charges.

---

## Step 4: Download Template

Download the category-specific template.

---

## Step 5: Complete Template

Fill all mandatory fields accurately.

Important:

- Use correct SKU information.
- Use correct size and fabric details.
- Use correct pricing.
- Incorrect information may result in higher returns.

---

## Step 6: Upload File

1. Save the completed template.
2. Click Add Files.
3. Upload the completed file.

---

## Step 7: Submit Catalog

1. Review all details.
2. Submit the catalog.
3. Wait for catalog approval.

---

## Best Practices

- Use accurate product information.
- Select the correct category.
- Upload high-quality product images.
- Avoid incorrect pricing.
- Include multiple products in a catalog where appropriate.
- Follow the template format exactly.
- Review all information before submission.
