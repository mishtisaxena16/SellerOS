# Meesho Ads Guide

## Overview

Meesho Ads is an advertising program that helps sellers promote their product catalogs to increase visibility and orders.

Advertised catalogs are shown more prominently across discovery and search pages, helping sellers reach more customers.

---

## How Meesho Ads Work

When a catalog is promoted through Meesho Ads:

- Products appear higher in discovery pages.
- Products become more visible to customers browsing similar items.
- Ads are displayed with an "Ad" label.
- Increased visibility can lead to more clicks and orders.

---

## Cost Model

Meesho Ads operates on a Cost Per Click (CPC) model.

Sellers are charged only when a customer performs an interaction such as:

- Clicking the catalog
- Opening the catalog details page
- Clicking share
- Clicking wishlist

If a customer only views the advertisement and performs no action, no advertising cost is charged.

Advertising costs are deducted from seller payouts based on actual budget utilization.

---

## Creating an Ad Campaign

### Step 1: Select Catalogs

Choose one or more catalogs to advertise.

Options include:

- Recommended catalogs
- Newly added catalogs
- Manually selected catalogs

Select catalogs that have strong sales potential and good product quality.

---

### Step 2: Set Campaign Budget

Choose either:

- Daily budget
- Total campaign budget

Budget represents the maximum amount that can be spent on advertising.

Best practice:

- Start with a daily budget.
- Monitor campaign performance regularly.
- Increase budget for high-performing campaigns.

---

### Step 3: Select Campaign Duration

Choose how long the campaign should run.

Recommendations:

- Use daily budget campaigns.
- Avoid setting short campaign durations.
- Monitor performance every few days.

A campaign ends when:

- End date is reached, or
- Allocated budget is exhausted.

---

## Campaign Configuration

### Campaign Name

Use descriptive campaign names that identify:

- Product category
- Product type
- Campaign objective

Example:

- Women's Kurti Campaign
- Home Decor Bestseller Campaign

---

### Recommended Catalogs

Meesho may suggest catalogs based on platform data and performance signals.

Sellers can:

- Use recommended catalogs
- Select catalogs manually

---

### CPC Settings

Sellers can:

- Use the recommended CPC
- Set a custom CPC value

For most campaigns, recommended CPC is suitable as a starting point.

---

## Campaign Monitoring

After launching a campaign, monitor performance through the Advertisement dashboard.

Important metrics include:

- ROI (Return on Investment)
- Budget utilization
- Click performance
- Campaign effectiveness

---

## Optimizing Performance

If a campaign shows positive ROI:

- Increase campaign budget.
- Continue running the campaign.
- Expand advertising to additional catalogs.

If performance is poor:

- Review product pricing.
- Improve product images.
- Improve product descriptions.
- Adjust campaign targeting and CPC.

---

## Best Practices

### Use High-Quality Catalogs

Advertise products that:

- Have good images
- Have accurate descriptions
- Are competitively priced

---

### Monitor ROI Regularly

Review campaign performance frequently and make adjustments based on results.

---

### Scale Winning Campaigns

Increase budget for campaigns that consistently generate orders and positive returns.

---

### Use Recommended CPC

Recommended CPC values are based on platform data and are often a good starting point for new advertisers.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Meesho Ads increases product visibility.
- Ads operate on a CPC model.
- Sellers pay only when customers interact with advertisements.
- Budget and campaign duration can be customized.
- ROI should be monitored regularly.
- Successful campaigns can be scaled through higher budgets.
