FOLLOW THESE GUIDELINES FOR FASTER APPROVALS:
 DESCRIPTION : Kindly include all the details required for your category
which the customer would need to know before buying. Sharing wrong
description will lead to more returns.
 CATEGORY : Upload the products from the same category that you have
chosen.
 PRODUCTS : Include at least 6 products in the catalog.
 PRICES : Enter final selling prices inclusive of commission and GST.
Don’t include shipping because the seller is not charged for shipping.
 FILES : It is recommended you follow the template shared by Meesho.
 IMAGES : Make sure you are sharing product images, either directly or
through a link in the Excel template.
