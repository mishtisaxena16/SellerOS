# Meesho Seller Troubleshooting Guide
## Purpose
This document contains common seller problems, possible causes, diagnosis steps, and recommended resolutions. It is intended for retrieval by the SellerOS Listing Agent, Analytics Agent, Inventory Agent, and Seller Coach.
---

# 1. Catalog Rejection Issues

## Problem
My catalog was rejected.

### Possible Reasons
- Missing mandatory attributes
- Incorrect category selection
- Low-quality product images
- Watermarked images
- Duplicate catalog
- Incorrect product information
- Pricing inconsistencies
- Restricted keywords
- Brand mismatch

### Resolution
1. Verify mandatory attributes.
2. Upload high-resolution images.
3. Remove duplicate listings.
4. Choose the correct category.
5. Ensure brand information is accurate.
6. Re-submit the catalog.

---

## Problem
My catalog passed but later became inactive.

### Possible Reasons
- Policy violation
- Restricted product
- Copyright complaint
- Product quality complaints
- Seller account issue

### Resolution
- Review policy notifications.
- Correct listing information.
- Contact Seller Support if required.

# 2. Image Issues

## Problem: Images were rejected.

### Possible Reasons
- Blurry images
- Low resolution
- Watermarks
- Text overlays
- Wrong background
- Cropped product
- Multiple products in one image

### Resolution
- Use clear high-resolution images.
- Use plain background.
- Remove watermarks.
- Show only the actual product.
- Follow Meesho image guidelines.

# 3. Inventory Issues

## Problem: Inventory is not updating.

### Possible Reasons
- Manual update not saved
- Bulk upload failed
- Wrong SKU
- Variant mismatch

### Resolution
- Verify SKU.
- Retry inventory upload.

# Common Questions

### Why was my catalog rejected?
Check mandatory attributes, images, category selection, pricing, and policy compliance.

### Why is my payment delayed?
Check settlement cycle, bank verification, and deductions.

### Why aren't my ads performing?
Review budget, bid, listing quality, and keywords.

### Why are orders getting cancelled?
Review inventory accuracy and dispatch timelines.

### Why is my account health poor?
Check cancellation rate, returns, complaints, and late shipments.

### Why can't I list this product?
Verify that it is not part of Meesho's restricted or prohibited products policy.
