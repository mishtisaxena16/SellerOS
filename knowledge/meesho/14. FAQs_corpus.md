# Returns & RTO FAQs

## What is the difference between Return and RTO?

Return:
Customer receives and returns the product.

RTO:
Delivery fails and shipment returns to seller.

---

## Can I reject a returned shipment?

No.

All shipments should be accepted according to the return SOP.

---

## What is a tampered shipment?

A shipment may be considered tampered when:

- Outer packaging is damaged
- Additional tape is present
- Labels appear altered

---

## What should I do if a shipment is tampered?

- Accept shipment
- Mark as Tampered/Disputed
- Record evidence
- Raise claim if necessary

---

## What is EPOD?

Electronic Proof of Delivery used during shipment acceptance and claims.

---

## How long do I have to raise a claim?

Claims should be raised within the applicable claim window.

---

## What evidence is required?

- Unboxing video
- Product images
- Barcode visibility
- AWB visibility

---

## Why was my claim rejected?

Possible reasons:

- Missing evidence
- Policy non-compliance
- Incomplete documentation
