# Meesho Barcoded Packaging Guide

## Overview

Barcoded Packaging is a Meesho packaging solution designed to protect sellers against courier fraud, wrong returns, and Wrong RTO (Return to Origin) claims. It uses tamper-proof packets with unique barcodes that are scanned during pickup and delivery.

---

# Purpose

Barcoded Packaging helps sellers:

- Protect shipments from courier fraud.
- Reduce Wrong RTO cases.
- Reduce Wrong Return cases.
- Improve RTO claim approvals.
- Improve delivery security.
- Enable order tracking through barcode scanning.

---

# Key Features

- 52-micron tamper-proof packaging.
- Unique barcode printed on every packet.
- Barcode scanned during pickup and delivery.
- Supports transparent and non-transparent packaging.
- Automatic verification through barcode scanning.
- Reusable packaging for cancelled orders through barcode relinking.

---

# Benefits

Using Barcoded Packaging can provide:

- Protection against courier fraud.
- Reduced Wrong RTO incidents.
- Reduced Wrong Return incidents.
- Higher RTO claim approval rates.
- Secure order handling.
- Improved shipment traceability.
- Better customer delivery experience.

---

# Scanning Process

Every packed order should be scanned before shipment.

Supported scanning methods:

## Desktop

- Connect a USB 2D QR barcode scanner.
- Scan the package barcode through the Meesho Seller Panel.

## Smartphone

- Open the Meesho Seller Panel in a mobile browser.
- Scan the barcode using the mobile device.

---

# Packaging Guidelines

To receive maximum protection:

- Pack every order using Meesho-approved barcoded packaging.
- Scan every order before dispatch.
- Ensure the barcode is clearly visible.
- Do not damage or cover the barcode.
- Use the correct packet size for the product.

---

# Transparent Barcoded Packaging

Recommended for most product categories.

Benefits include:

- Protection against courier fraud.
- Better Wrong RTO prevention.
- Improved claim approval rates.
- Easy barcode scanning.
- Shipment verification during pickup and delivery.

---

# Non-Transparent Barcoded Packaging

Sensitive product categories should use non-transparent barcoded packets.

These packets provide:

- Privacy for sensitive products.
- Barcode-based shipment protection.
- Compliance with Meesho packaging requirements.

---

# Barcode Verification

Each packet contains a unique barcode.

The barcode is scanned:

- Before shipment pickup.
- During delivery.

Benefits:

- Prevents fake packet replacement.
- Confirms package authenticity.
- Improves shipment traceability.
- Supports automatic claim verification.

Packets that cannot be scanned may not be eligible for protection.

---

# Reusing Packaging

Packaging from cancelled orders may be reused by relinking the barcode through the Meesho Seller Panel, where supported.

---

# RTO & Return Protection

Using approved Barcoded Packaging helps:

- Reduce Wrong RTO.
- Reduce Wrong Returns.
- Increase successful RTO claim approvals.
- Improve fraud detection.
- Protect seller shipments throughout delivery.

---

# Claim Eligibility

To remain eligible for protection:

- Use approved Meesho Barcoded Packaging.
- Scan every shipment.
- Follow Meesho packaging guidelines.
- Purchase packaging from authorized vendors.

Failure to comply may affect claim eligibility.

---

Meesho recommends purchasing packaging only from authorized vendors.

Examples include:

- Parin Polymers (Chandigarh)
- Krishna Polymers (Ajmer)
- Pacfo (Surat)
- VS Products (Delhi NCR)

- Shri Anand Polymers (Gurgaon)
- Global Parachem (Ghaziabad)
- Euphoria Packaging (Ahmedabad)
- Maruthi Plastics (Chennai)

Using unauthorized vendors may result in:

- RTO claim blocking.
- Return claim blocking.
- Account policy violations.
- Possible account suspension.

---

# Best Practices

- Pack 100% of eligible orders using Barcoded Packaging.
- Scan every package before dispatch.
- Use transparent packets unless the product requires non-transparent packaging.
- Purchase packets only from authorized vendors.
- Ensure barcodes remain clean and readable.
- Replace damaged packaging before shipment.

---

Barcoded Packaging is a security feature that protects Meesho sellers against courier fraud, Wrong RTOs, and return disputes. Proper use of approved barcoded packets, barcode scanning, and authorized vendors helps improve claim approval rates, shipment security, and overall seller protection.
