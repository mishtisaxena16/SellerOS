# Revised SOP for Return & RTO Shipment Acceptance

**Effective Date:** 15 March 2023

Meesho has introduced a revised Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to address issues related to tampered Return and RTO shipments delivered by logistics partners. The objective is to improve supplier protection while maintaining a standardized claim process.

---

# Revised Return & RTO Acceptance Policy

## 1. Mandatory Shipment Acceptance

Suppliers must accept **every Return and RTO shipment**, regardless of whether the packet appears tampered or intact.

Rejecting any shipment is not permitted under this SOP.

---

## 2. Mark Tampered Shipments

If a shipment is received in a tampered condition and the product inside is:

- Wrong product
- Damaged
- Used
- Missing quantity

the supplier must record the remark:

> **Tampered / Disputed**

on the Electronic Proof of Delivery (EPOD/POD) while accepting the shipment.

---

## 3. Raise a Claim After Delivery

After accepting the shipment, suppliers may raise a claim if the received product is:

- Wrong
- Damaged
- Used
- Quantity missing

Claims should be raised using the appropriate ticket disposition on the Supplier Panel.

---

## 4. Shipment Condition Selection

When raising a claim, suppliers must upload:

- Suborder Number
- Unboxing Video
- Shipment Condition

### Available Shipment Condition Options

### Tampered

Select **Tampered** if:

- Shipment was received in tampered condition.
- "Tampered/Disputed" remark was mentioned on the POD during delivery.

### Intact

Select **Intact** if:

- Shipment was received without any signs of tampering.

---

## 5. Claim Validation

After submission:

- Logistics partners validate the evidence.
- Eligible claims are processed according to Meesho policy.

---

# Important Policy Notes

## Shipment Rejection

If a supplier rejects any Return/RTO shipment:

- Shipment will be disposed of by the logistics partner.
- Product will not be delivered.
- Supplier becomes ineligible for any refund or claim.

---

## RTO Claim Eligibility

Suppliers are eligible for RTO claims only if:

- Meesho branded packaging is used during dispatch.

Orders dispatched using non-Meesho packaging are:

- Not eligible for RTO claims.
- Considered **Intact** by default.

---

## Claim Submission Deadline

Claims must be raised:

**Within 7 days of shipment delivery.**

---

## OTP Verified Delivery

Suppliers should follow the OTP verified delivery process wherever available to improve shipment tracking and digital Proof of Delivery.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is considered a Tampered Shipment?

A shipment is considered **Tampered** if:

- Outer packaging is visibly torn.
- Packaging is damaged.
- Packet is in poor condition.
- Additional tape is present (except logistics partner branded tape).
- Shipping label appears cut and pasted from another shipment.

---

## 2. What is NOT considered Tampered?

A shipment is **not** considered tampered if:

- Outer packaging has no visible damage.
- Packaging is intact.
- No unauthorized taping is present.
- Only the forward shipping label is missing.

---

## 3. What should suppliers do when receiving a tampered shipment?

Suppliers should:

1. Accept the shipment.
2. Mention **Tampered/Disputed** on the POD.
3. Record an unboxing video.
4. Raise a claim on the Supplier Panel.

If no tampered remark is recorded on the POD, Meesho will assume the shipment was received intact.

---

## 4. How to Raise a Claim

### Step 1

Open the **Support** section on the Supplier Panel.

Select the appropriate ticket issue.

---

### Step 2

Enter:

- Suborder Number
- Product Images
- Waybill Image
- Unboxing Video

Choose the shipment condition:

- Tampered
- Intact

---

### Step 3

Submit the ticket.

---

## 5. Evidence Required for Claims

Suppliers should provide:

- Unboxing video
- Product images
- Shipment images
- Waybill image
- Correct shipment condition

---

# Unboxing Video Guidelines

The video must satisfy all of the following:

- Package must be opened during recording.
- Package should not be pre-opened.
- Show all sides of the package.
- Clearly highlight damaged/tampered portions.
- Barcode must remain visible.
- AWB Number and Order ID must be clearly visible.
- Entire recording should be continuous without cuts.
- Camera movement should be slow and stable for clarity.

Failure to meet these requirements may result in claim rejection.

---

## 6. What Happens if a Supplier Rejects a Shipment?

If a supplier rejects a shipment:

- Logistics partner marks it as **Disposed**.
- Supplier cannot raise any claim.
- No dispute or refund is allowed.

Therefore, suppliers should always accept Return and RTO shipments and raise disputes afterward if necessary.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Accept every Return/RTO shipment.
- Never reject tampered packets.
- Mark **Tampered/Disputed** on POD whenever applicable.
- Record a continuous unboxing video.
- Raise claims within 7 days.
- Use Meesho branded packaging to remain eligible for RTO claims.
- Submit proper evidence for successful claim processing.
