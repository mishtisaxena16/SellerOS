# Uploading Catalogs

This guide explains how to upload product catalogs on the Meesho Supplier Panel using two available methods.

---

# Catalog Upload Process

1. Go to **Catalogs Upload**.
2. Click **Add New Catalog**.
3. Fill in all required details:
   - Product Description
   - Category
   - Images
   - Files (Excel Template)
   - Image Links (if applicable)
4. Submit the catalog.

---

# General Guidelines

Follow these guidelines for faster catalog approval:

## Product Description

Include all relevant product details that customers need before purchasing, such as:

- Material
- Colour
- Pattern
- Size / Dimensions
- Type
- Product features
- Any additional relevant information

Providing incorrect or incomplete descriptions may increase product returns.

---

## Category Selection

- Upload products only under the correct category.
- All products in one catalog should belong to the same category.
- If unsure about the category, contact your Business Development Manager (BDM).

---

## Catalog Size

- Minimum recommended products: **6**
- For image-based upload:
  - Minimum: **4 products**
  - Maximum: **18 products**

Uploading more than six products is recommended for faster approval.

---

## Pricing Guidelines

The selling price entered should:

- Include Meesho commission.
- Include GST.
- Exclude shipping charges (shipping is not charged separately to sellers).

The catalog starting price should always be the **lowest selling price** among all products in the catalog.

---

## Images

- Upload clear product images.
- Images may be uploaded directly or linked through the Excel template.
- Ensure images correspond to the correct products.

---

## Files

Meesho recommends using the official Excel template for product uploads.

---

# Method 1: Upload Using Description and Images

## Step 1: Enter Product Description

Include:

- Material
- Colour
- Pattern
- Sizes / Measurements
- Product Type
- Selling Price (inclusive of GST and Meesho commission)
- Other relevant information

If products have different prices, mention this clearly in the description.

---

## Step 2: Select Category

Choose the correct product category.

If unsure, contact your BDM.

---

## Step 3: Enter Catalog Starting Price

Enter the lowest selling price in the catalog.

This price should already include:

- GST
- Meesho commission

---

## Step 4: Upload Images

1. Click **Add Images**.
2. Click **Open Dropzone**.
3. Select all product images.
4. Click **Submit Catalog**.

---

# Method 2: Upload Using Excel Template

## Step 1: Fill Product Description

Provide the catalog description as specified in the Excel template.

---

## Step 2: Select Category

Choose the appropriate category.

If unsure, contact your BDM.

---

## Step 3: Enter Catalog Starting Price

Enter the lowest selling price in the catalog.

The price must include:

- GST
- Meesho commission

---

## Step 4: Download Template

Download the official Meesho product upload template.

---

## Step 5: Complete the Excel Template

Fill all required product information.

### Required Information

- SKU ID
- Product Name
- Fabric / Material
- Size Chart
- Price
- Category
- Product Images
- Other mandatory attributes

Incorrect information may increase returns or lead to catalog rejection.

---

## Excel Upload Guidelines

- Provide accurate SKU IDs.
- Enter correct fabric/material details.
- Upload the correct size chart.
- Enter accurate pricing.
- Selling price must include GST and Meesho commission.
- Do not include shipping charges.
- Upload more than six products whenever possible.
- Use the category list provided in the Excel template.

---

## Step 6: Upload the Excel File

After saving the completed template:

1. Click **Add Files**.

---

## Step 7: Open Dropzone

Click **Open Dropzone**.

---

## Step 8: Select the Excel File

Choose the completed Excel file from your computer.

Click **Submit**.

---

## Step 9: Submit Catalog

Click **Submit Catalog**.

The catalog will then be uploaded for review.

---

# Best Practices

- Use the official Excel template whenever possible.
- Provide complete and accurate product information.
- Upload products under the correct category.
- Include high-quality product images.
- Ensure pricing includes GST and Meesho commission.
- Exclude shipping charges from product prices.
- Upload at least six products for faster processing.
- Double-check SKU IDs, sizes, and product attributes before submission.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Two catalog upload methods are available:
  - Description + Images
  - Excel Template
- Select the correct category.
- Use accurate product descriptions.
- Upload clear product images.
- Use the official Excel template for bulk uploads.
- Include GST and Meesho commission in selling prices.
- Do not include shipping costs.
- Submit complete and accurate product information to avoid delays or rejections.
