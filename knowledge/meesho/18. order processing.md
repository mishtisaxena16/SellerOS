# Order Processing

This guide explains the complete order processing workflow on the Meesho Supplier Panel, from accepting orders to shipment pickup.

---

# Order Management Overview

Navigate to **Orders** in the Supplier Panel to manage all order stages.

The Orders section contains:

- **Pending** – Newly received orders awaiting acceptance.
- **To Be Dispatched** – Accepted orders awaiting shipment.
- **Ready to Ship** – Orders ready for courier pickup.
- **Shipped** – Orders picked up by the courier.
- **Cancelled** – Orders cancelled by either the seller or the customer.

You can also:

- Download shipping labels.
- Download shipment manifests.
- Accept orders.
- Select expected dispatch dates.

---

# Order Processing Workflow

## Step 1: Open Orders

Go to:

**Orders**

to view all incoming and existing orders.

---

## Step 2: View Pending Orders

Open the **Pending** section to see all newly received orders awaiting acceptance.

---

## Step 3: Select Orders

Choose the orders you want to process.

You can use **Select All Orders** to accept multiple orders at once.

---

## Step 4: Accept Orders

Click **Accept Selected Orders**.

---

## Step 5: Select Expected Dispatch Date

Choose the expected dispatch date and click **Submit**.

### Dispatch Policy

- Orders should be dispatched within **2 days** of acceptance.
- After submission, accepted orders move to the **To Be Dispatched** section.

---

## Step 6: View Orders to Be Dispatched

Open the **To Be Dispatched** section to view accepted orders waiting for packaging and shipment.

---

## Step 7: Mark Orders as Ready to Ship

After packaging the products, click **Ready to Ship**.

The orders will move to the **Ready to Ship** section.

---

## Step 8: Download Shipping Documents

For every shipment, download:

- Shipping Label
- Shipment Manifest

These documents must accompany the package before handing it over to the courier.

---

# Product Packaging Guidelines

Proper packaging helps reduce returns, RTOs, and customer complaints.

## Packaging Material

Products should be packed using:

- Plain white polyester packets
- Cartons (recommended for fragile products)

---

## Branding Restrictions

Do **not** expose:

- Your brand name
- Your branding
- MRP labels
- Packaging that reveals your selling price

Since Meesho resellers sell products at different prices, supplier branding should remain hidden.

---

## MRP Guidelines

Never use packaging that displays the product's Maximum Retail Price (MRP).

---

## Packaging Quality

Ensure products are securely packed to reduce:

- Damage during transit
- Returns
- Return to Origin (RTO)
- Poor customer ratings

---

## Plastic Ban Advisory

Meesho recommends using environmentally friendly packaging materials such as:

- Corrugated sheets
- Paper bags
- Water-resistant high-GSM paper envelopes
- Other approved alternatives

Suppliers are responsible for any legal issues, penalties, or shipment detention resulting from the use of prohibited plastic packaging.

---

# Shipment Pickup

Once the courier collects the shipment, the order status automatically moves to the **Shipped** section.

---

# Cancelled Orders

Orders cancelled by either:

- Seller
- Customer

will appear in the **Cancelled** section.

---

# Best Practices

- Accept orders promptly.
- Dispatch orders within 2 days.
- Package products securely.
- Hide supplier branding and MRP labels.
- Download and attach the correct shipping label and manifest.
- Use recommended packaging materials.
- Hand over shipments to the courier on time.

# Key Takeaways

- Review new orders under **Pending**.
- Accept orders and select an expected dispatch date.
- Dispatch orders within **2 days**.
- Package products according to Meesho guidelines.
- Hide branding and MRP labels.
- Download the shipping label and manifest before courier pickup.
- Orders automatically move to **Shipped** after courier collection.
- Cancelled orders are tracked separately under **Cancelled**.
