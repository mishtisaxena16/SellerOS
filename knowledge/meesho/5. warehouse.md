# Meesho Warehouse

## Overview

Meesho Warehouse is a fulfillment service that allows sellers to store inventory in Meesho-operated warehouses. The service is designed to improve order fulfillment, increase delivery speed, reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) and return-related losses, and provide sellers with real-time inventory visibility.

---

## Warehouse Onboarding

To join the Meesho Warehouse program:

1. Register as a Meesho seller.
2. Apply to join the Meesho Warehouse program.
3. Complete GST and Additional Place of Business (APOB) registration.
4. Dispatch inventory to the assigned warehouse.
5. Start fulfilling customer orders through Meesho Warehouse.

---

## Benefits

Using Meesho Warehouse provides the following benefits:

- Increase sales by approximately **9–11%**.
- Protection against eligible RTO and return fraud claims.
- Access to Meesho customer discounts without affecting seller margins.
- Reduce returns, RTOs, and cancellations by approximately **6–7%**.
- Professional warehouse handling to minimize product damage.
- Real-time inventory tracking.
- Warehouse performance insights.
- Streamlined fulfillment operations.

## Reverse Shipping Fees

Reverse shipping charges depend on:

- Courier partner
- Shipment weight
- Shipping method (Self Ship or Warehouse)

Supported courier partners include:

- Valmo
- Xpressbees
- Shadowfax
- Delhivery
- Ecom Express

Refer to the latest Meesho warehouse pricing schedule for courier-specific reverse shipping charges.

## Best Practices

- Complete GST and APOB registration before applying.
- Maintain accurate warehouse inventory records.
- Monitor inventory levels regularly.
- Follow Meesho warehouse guidelines for packaging and dispatch.
- Review warehouse fee structures periodically to understand operational costs.
- Track warehouse performance metrics to improve fulfillment efficiency.
