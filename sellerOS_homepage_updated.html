How SellerOS Works

From physical chaos
to autonomous order.

The Physical World
Orchestrated by software.
01

Connect your marketplaces

Link Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho in minutes. SellerOS authenticates through the unified Connector Layer — no scripts, no spreadsheets.

02

Import & understand products

Listings, inventory and orders sync automatically. The Supervisor builds a live model of your entire catalog and performance.

03

Agents analyze the data

The Orchestrator dispatches work to specialized agents — pricing, forecasting, ads — each reasoning over your real business context.

04

Tasks flow to marketplaces

Actions route through the Tool Layer and Connector Layer straight to marketplace APIs. Agents never touch a raw endpoint.

05

You receive decisions

SellerOS surfaces clear recommendations and executed actions. You approve strategy — the AI handles the repetitive execution.
